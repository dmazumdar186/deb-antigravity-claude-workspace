Renewable Power Generation Lead – Ireland

Location: Dublin, Ireland
Job Type: Permanent
Sector: Energy & Renewable Power

The Opportunity

A leading multidisciplinary engineering consultancy is seeking an experienced Renewable Power Generation Lead to provide senior leadership across the Irish energy market.

This is a strategic role focused on strengthening market presence, developing key client relationships, supporting major project delivery and building renewable energy capability in Ireland. The successful candidate will work across a broad portfolio of renewable power generation opportunities, with particular focus on offshore wind, onshore wind, solar and battery energy storage systems (BESS).

Key Responsibilities

  • Provide senior technical and strategic leadership across the Irish Renewable Power Generation market.
  • Build and maintain trusted relationships with key clients and industry stakeholders.
  • Strengthen the organisation's presence and reputation across Ireland's energy sector.
  • Lead and support the delivery of major renewable energy engineering projects.
  • Identify market opportunities and contribute to business development and growth strategies.
  • Represent the organisation at relevant renewable energy, offshore engineering and industry forums.
  • Develop local engineering capability through recruitment, mentoring, coaching and knowledge sharing.
  • Work collaboratively with UK and international renewable energy teams to support a coordinated approach.
  • Collaborate with planning, consenting, project management, environmental, transmission and distribution teams.
  • Provide strategic input into client engagement, project pursuits and technical proposals.

About You

  • Degree qualified in an appropriate engineering discipline.
  • Chartered Engineer or equivalent professional qualification.
  • Significant experience leading energy engineering projects.
  • Strong understanding of the Renewable Power Generation sector and Irish energy policy environment.
  • Experience across one or more areas such as offshore wind, onshore wind, solar PV or BESS.
  • Proven ability to develop senior client relationships and identify commercial opportunities.
  • Strong technical leadership, communication and stakeholder management skills.
  • Experience working across multidisciplinary and geographically diverse teams.
  • Commercially aware with a strong understanding of the Irish energy market.

This is an opportunity to take a senior leadership position within Ireland's evolving renewable energy sector, helping shape market growth, develop technical capability and support the delivery of major clean energy projects.
