On the Jeevana yoga site (and calm wellness sites generally) use no black or near-black for buttons, bands or sections; use terracotta, sand and warm deep brown, keeping text contrast at 4.5:1 or better.

Why: Operator 2026-11-14 on the revamp: 'I don't prefer to have black on a yoga website, be it in buttons or in sections'.
