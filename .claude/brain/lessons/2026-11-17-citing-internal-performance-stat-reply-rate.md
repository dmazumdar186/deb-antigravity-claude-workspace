Before citing an internal performance stat (reply rate, lift) in a pitch or decision, re-derive it from raw data with confidence intervals and a confound check; a number in a note is a hypothesis, not evidence.

Why: 2026-11-17: a '1.31% vs 0.38% trigger lift' from an old note was repeated in pitches for days; re-derived from 92,810 sends it was a vertical split, clustered lift 0.70x [0.50-0.97].
