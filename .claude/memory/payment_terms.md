# Payment terms — standing rule (operator order, 2026-10-31)

**50% of the agreed fee on signature, before any work starts; 50% on acceptance, before go-live.** No exceptions, no milestone splits smaller than this, no "pay at the end". Reason: three clients underpaid or stalled (one paid <20% after 4 months of chasing; one paid a token amount after a full site + dashboard + reviews + SEO was delivered; one still not agreeing).

Mechanics that always accompany it (proposal/contract, not the email):
- Acceptance = the client's own written test list, frozen at signature; deemed accepted if no written defect list within 5 working days of staging delivery.
- Go-live, rights to the new code/design, and admin/deploy access pass to the client only on cleared final payment. Their existing accounts and data stay theirs throughout.
- Deposit is non-refundable and covers work to date. Late client inputs shift dates day for day; >10 working days pauses the project (restart fee); >30 days makes work done so far due.
- Every change quoted in writing and paid before it starts; a new decision-maker reopening an approved milestone is a change.
- Invoices due 7 days; French late-payment penalties (art. L441-10 Code de commerce, 40 € indemnity D441-5 — verify with the accountant); suspension at D+15; payer bears bank fees.
- Monthly plans invoiced at the start of each month, suspended on non-payment after notice.
