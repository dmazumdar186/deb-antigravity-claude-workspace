## 3. Reach mechanics — how a post travels 1st → 2nd → 3rd degree

LinkedIn shows your post to a sample of your 1st-degree; if they engage *fast*, it expands to their
networks (2nd degree), and so on. You engineer for that first-hour engagement. **Honest caveat:
reach is algorithm-dependent — none of this guarantees virality; it stacks the odds.**

- **Hook does 90% of the work.** Line 1 must stop the scroll on its own. Lead with a number, a
  tension, or a blunt claim — never "I'm excited to announce."
- **The first 60–90 minutes decide the ceiling — and it starts with YOU.** Reply to every comment
  within ~10 minutes; your own early activity signals the algorithm to expand reach (comments >
  likes). Post when your network is actually online (check your analytics for the active window).
  End some posts with a question to bait comments.
- **Dwell time.** Whitespace + short lines keep people on the post longer → the algorithm reads
  that as quality.
- **Reshares are the multiplier.** T7 (VC/referral) and T3 (build-in-public) are the most
  shareable — that's how you jump to 2nd/3rd degree. Make them easy to reshare with a clean hook.
- **Tag with intent, not spam.** Tag a real person only when genuinely relevant (a client who'll
  vouch, a peer who'll engage). One or two, max.
- **Comment → DM funnel.** Anyone who comments something buyer-shaped gets a DM (script below).
- **Link placement — test it, don't dogma it.** The old "external link in the body kills reach"
  rule has eased (LinkedIn dialed the penalty back in 2024–25), and a link in the first comment
  can cost click-throughs (fewer people expand comments). Run both across a few posts and let the
  UTM data decide.
- **The biggest lever isn't your posts — it's your comments.** Spend **15 minutes a day** leaving
  substantive comments on your ICP's posts and 10–15 relevant creators. For a 5k account this is
  the single highest-ROI move for reaching 2nd/3rd degree. Line up 5–8 trusted peers who reliably
  engage in your first hour (a small pod) — their early comments push you into *their* networks.

## 4. Four-week content calendar (concrete, ~3×/week)

Anchor to a Monday. Rotate audience + angle so no two consecutive posts feel the same.

| Wk | Mon | Wed | Fri |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Launch post** (pin it) | T3 build-in-public (founder) | T1 proof-story (SME) |
| 2 | T4 teardown (Head of Product) | T9 SME-pain (SME) | T7 VC/referral (VC) |
| 3 | T2 outcome/ROI (ops) | T8 founder-pain (founder) | T5 the offer (all) |
| 4 | T10 what-I-use (technical) | T6 objection-killer (founder) | Client win / testimonial (reuse Jitendra: "caught a bug killing 83% of outbound leads… I'd hire him again tomorrow") |

Then loop, swapping fresh proof/examples. One FR post per week for the Paris slice.

**The non-negotiables that actually make the reach math fire (the campaign fails here, not on
weak content — both panelists):**
- **Every day, post day or not: 15 minutes of strategic commenting** on ICP + creator posts.
- **Reply to every comment within the golden hour; DM buyer-shaped engagers the same day.**
- **Repost your best performer every ~10 days** — don't let a winner die after one run.
- Skip the follow-through and posts land flat — then you wrongly conclude "LinkedIn doesn't work."
  Block the 15 minutes like a meeting.

**Audience-fit note (panel):** for the SME cut (T1, T2, T9) lead with concrete chores + time/
hours saved, **not** the $1M-pipeline / $200k-SDR / 48k-emails numbers — those read agency-scale
to a 30-person business and trigger "not for me." Save the big money numbers for founder/VC posts
where scale is the flex.

## 6. Lead magnet (the step between "scroll past" and "book a call")

Not everyone books on the first touch. Give the warm-but-not-hot lead a low-commitment yes:

> **Free "AI Opportunity Audit"** — send me one sentence about your business and I'll reply with
> the 3 highest-ROI things I'd automate first, and roughly what each saves. No call required.

Delivered by DM or a short Loom. It captures the lead, shows the thinking, and warms them toward
the paid build. Mention it in T2, T9, and the DM script.
