# prodcraft.fyi — promo additions (self-qualifier + cross-links)

The site already converts well (hero, proof bar, systems, "how we work", free-call CTA). Two
additions sharpen it: a **"who this is for / not for"** block (makes the right buyer feel seen and
filters tire-kickers), and **cross-links** to Upwork/Malt/LinkedIn for buyers who prefer a platform.

> These are copy blocks. Wiring them into `portfolio_site/` is a **live-site edit — flagged, needs
> your go-ahead** (and your real Upwork/Malt slugs). Content files live in
> `execution/personal_workflows/portfolio_site/src/content/*.en.json`.

---

## Block 1 — "Who this is for" (drop between Services and Systems)

**Eyebrow:** Is this you?
**Title:** Built for founders and teams who need it shipped — not staffed.

**This is for you if:**
- You have an AI feature or automation you need **live in weeks**, not a six-month roadmap item.
- You want **one senior operator** who scopes it and builds it — not an agency with five handoffs.
- You'd rather **own the code and move fast** than hire a full-time AI engineer.
- You care about the **outcome** (more meetings, hours back, a shipped product), not the tech stack.

**This isn't for you if:**
- You want the cheapest possible hourly dev.
- You need a 6-month enterprise procurement cycle.
- You're looking for a co-founder for equity.

*(One senior operator. Your code and credentials, day one. Live in weeks.)*
