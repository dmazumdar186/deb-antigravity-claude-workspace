A note on this concept.

What this is

This is an unofficial redesign concept of misb.fr, hosted on my own domain. Nothing touches your site, your hosting or your data. Every fact on the page — program names, RNCP 38666, fees, addresses, accreditation wording, the six testimonials and their attribution, the blog titles — is copied from the public English pages of misb.fr as they stood on 15 October 2026. Where your site publishes something two ways, I picked one and footnoted it. Where it publishes nothing (intake dates, partner names, founding year), the concept says "to wire" rather than inventing.

It is a single static page plus this note: no framework, no third-party scripts, self-hosted fonts, about 4 MB of your own photography, and the hero leaflet is pure CSS 3D.

What I found on your site

Six things, written as opportunities rather than complaints. All observed on the live English pages.

  • Template leftovers are visible. A live gallery page reads "Details about old-lyon in lyon gallery will go here", the Mastère page prints a raw {{ route(…) }} placeholder as a link target, and an appointment button says "appointment.form.loading_slots". Ten minutes of cleanup removes the impression of an unfinished site.
  • Copy from another school. The Enterprise Office page calls MISB "a leading business school in the Yvelines", the rights page lists a mediation address at mantes-school.com, and the Lyon appointment button points at a hostingersite.com URL. None of these are MISB.
  • Numbers contradict each other. "+100 partner companies / +50 years of experience" on the Career Center page versus "+3 companies / +5 years" on the Partner page; the work-study rhythm is stated three ways (2/3, 3/2, 1/4); the Lyon address appears as Villette 69003, Villeurbanne and Paris 75015; Bachelor RNCP fees differ by specialisation with no explanation. A prospective student or parent will notice.
  • Typos in the trust copy. "Our Acrediation", "Academic Qualitication", "Petitt Palais", "alue Proposition", "Student's Say". These sit exactly where credibility is decided.
  • The hero is a 10 MB video. On a phone on 4G that is the whole first impression. Your own photo albums are stronger and lighter; the concept uses them at under 200 KB each.
  • Lyon is almost absent. One classroom photo, three empty albums, and a campus whose "MISB Lyon ouvre ses portes !" post is dated 27 Feb 2026 and which "welcomes students from over 60 nationalities" — but no imagery to show for it. The entrance sign alone makes a good hero panel; a half-day shoot would fill the rest.

What doesn't exist yet

  • A consistent social content calendar. Six social profiles are linked in the header and two icons point nowhere; the Instagram handle misb.fr is the natural home for the Thursday visit program.
  • Lyon photography. Campus interior, the Part-Dieu neighbourhood, the first cohorts — nothing is published beyond one classroom.
  • Named partner proof. Five unlabelled logos and a "Business Club" with no members listed. Even three named companies with a one-line quote would do more than "+100".
  • Intake dates. "Spring / Autumn" is all that is published. A next-start date and an application deadline are the two facts every applicant looks for first.
  • Outcome data. The RNCP page says "Data not yet available — first cohort in progress", which is honest; a plan to publish the first cohort's results would turn that into a trust point.
