Alternance · Work-study

Train and gain real-world experience

Alternance is a training system that combines periods at school or a training centre with periods in a company. The student is a full employee with a work contract and a salary.

  • Work contractApprenticeship or professionalisation contract, CDD or sometimes CDI.
  • PaidAppropriate remuneration according to your apprenticeship year; paid leave like all employees.
  • Recognised diplomaA State diploma or RNCP certification — RNCP 38666 Level 6.
  • Company mentorA tutor in the company and follow-up by the school.
1day in class
4days in company — Bac +3 schedule, as stated by the Career Center2
  • Train with no tuition fees: Training costs are covered by the company and its OPCO.
  • Apprenticeship contract: ages 16 to 29. Professionalisation contract: 16 to 25, and job seekers 26+.
  • Rights: complete social coverage and support. Duties: work actively, follow MISB training, respect company and school rules.
  • Mobility: study or intern at another institution, often abroad — academic exchanges from a few weeks to a full year.

Admissions

MISB Admission Process Guide

Application file, individual interview, admission outside of Parcoursup. Late entry possible up to 4 weeks after the start of training.

Online application

Apply at misb.fr. Complete the application form with your personal, academic, and program information. Upload the required documents (passport, diploma, CV, cover letter, etc.).

Evaluation & Interview

The Admissions Committee will review your application. If shortlisted, you will be contacted within 3–4 days to schedule a 15–20-minute interview via Google Meet.

Admission & Enrollment Formalities

If admitted, you will receive detailed instructions. International students: the school will provide the necessary documents to support your student visa application. Attend the orientation session upon arrival in Paris or Lyon.

IntakesSpringAutumnStart dates: to wire — not published on misb.fr. An application fee of €30 is charged for any admission application.
