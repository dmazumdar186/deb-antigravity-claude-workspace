LinkedIn · 05 / 10 · credentials

RNCP 38666. Qualiopi. Académie de Paris.

Three things a parent, an employer or a partner should know about MISB.
RNCP 38666: a Level 6 title registered with France Compétences and issued by the Ministry of Labour, prepared through the Bachelor's degree in Retail Establishment Management.
Qualiopi-certified, as stated by MISB: certification of the quality of training processes, issued by a body accredited by the French State.
Accredited by the Académie de Paris, as stated by MISB.
Établissement d'enseignement supérieur privé.
Certificates → https://www.misb.fr/en/Our-accreditation

LinkedIn · 06 / 10 · mastere-tracks

Six Mastère tracks.

The MISB Mastère (private diploma) runs two years, four semesters, face-to-face, 20 to 25 hours a week; direct entry in the second year is possible depending on profile.
Six tracks: International Business Management · Hotel and Tourism Management · Entrepreneurship and Startup Strategies · International Marketing · Human Resources Management · Luxury Brand Management.
Mondays and Tuesdays for theory, Wednesday workshops, Thursdays and Fridays for cultural and company visits.
Paris and Lyon. English or French.
Programme → https://www.misb.fr/en/Mastère

LinkedIn · 08 / 10 · bbs-dba-fle

Three more ways in.

Beyond the Bachelor RNCP and the Mastère, three further pathways at MISB.
BBS, Bachelor in Business Studies: three years, six semesters, post-Baccalaureate, a common core over two years then a specialisation.
DBA, Doctorate in Business Administration: three years, 24 modules, for executives, entrepreneurs and managers, entry at Bac+5, face-to-face or hybrid.
FLE, French as a foreign language: beginner to advanced, in person, 10 hours a week, 12 students per class, from four weeks.
BBS and DBA are private academic titles issued by MISB.
Programmes → https://www.misb.fr
