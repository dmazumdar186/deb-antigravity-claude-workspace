08.1 — What it is

A school prospectus you scroll through, not a brochure you scroll past.

Business-school prospectuses are designed documents: a dark cover, numbered chapters, one idea per spread, a fold-out of campus life, and a back page that tells you how to apply. The concept at the cover carries that DNA on the web. The cover is pinned and scrubbed by scroll — four beats, each a published fact — over a generative Paris → Lyon journey (a self-rendered film, with a live canvas as the fallback; the slot is ready for Kling footage). Chapter 01 is the fold-out, done properly: a tri-fold spread that opens as you scroll, Paris on the left, the five real photo albums in the middle, Lyon on the right. Chapters 02–07 are a command deck — one panel area, six tabs, deep-linkable — so programs, work-study, the Career Center, the Student HUB, admissions and contact each sit a single tap away, never a scroll away.

Everything written on the concept is copied verbatim from misb.fr as of 15 October 2026. Where the live site contradicts itself, the concept shows the published figures side by side and footnotes the contradiction rather than choosing one.

08.3 — Six findings, written as opportunities

What the live site already tells us.

  • Make the two campuses legible. Lyon is published at three addresses (75 Rue de la Villette 69003; 34 Rue Antoine Primat, Villeurbanne; a Paris head office) and Paris as both 75019 and 75015. One address per campus, one map link, one Metro line — the concept uses the footer addresses and says so.
  • Publish one work-study rhythm. The same Bac+3 pathway is described as 3 days company / 2 days college, 2 days school / 3 days company, and 1 day class / 4 days company. Apprentices and employers plan around this number; the concept footnotes all three until the school confirms one.
  • Reconcile the proof numbers. "+100 partner companies / +50 years of experience" on the Career Center page sits next to "+3 companies / +5 years" on the partner page, while the RNCP indicators say "first cohort in progress". Honest, consistent counters are a trust asset.
  • Explain the fee grid once. Bachelor RNCP fees differ by specialisation (€8 300 / €6 300 vs €5,100 / €4,700) without explanation, and the €30 / €300 fees live in the legal page. One fee table with the residence rule up front removes a question from every admissions call.
  • Retire the template leftovers. "leading business school in the Yvelines", a BTS schedule, a mediation email at another school's domain, a gym photo on the Lyon page, a Blade route printed as a link, "Details about old-lyon in lyon gallery will go here." Each is a one-line fix that protects the trust the accreditation copy earns.
  • Show real people, consistently. The gallery albums, the two directors and the six testimonials are the school's strongest assets; the program tiles are stock posters with French titles baked into the pixels. The concept uses only MISB photography as plates and keeps the testimonials verbatim.

08.4 — What doesn't exist yet

Honest gaps, so the concept stays honest.

  • A content calendar. The news feed runs 73 posts, mostly French pop-culture pieces on an English site; the institutional posts (graduation, Lyon opening, alternance launch) deserve a rhythm of their own.
  • Lyon photography. One campus interior and an entrance sign exist; the three other Lyon albums are empty placeholders. The fold-out's right panel is ready for a shoot.
  • Named partners. Five logos with alt text "Partner" and no company names. A named partner strip with a build-time assertion replaces the counters the moment the names are confirmed.
  • Intake dates. Only "Spring / Autumn" is published. A dated next intake turns the admissions chapter into a deadline.
  • A brochure PDF behind no form. The brochure sits behind a form; a direct PDF link is the single most-clicked prospectus action.
