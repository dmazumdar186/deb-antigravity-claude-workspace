# PM CV framing 2025-2026 (research notes; secondary sources, mostly resume-vendor blogs)
Caveat: no dedicated Lenny resume post, no apec.fr CV-format page, no Welcome to the Jungle page surfaced. Do not claim them.

## Bullets
- Formula: action verb + task + measurable result; "skill: accomplishment"; <=2 lines; 5-7 bullets for two most recent roles, 3-5 for older. https://www.aced.io/blog/how-to-write-the-perfect-product-manager-resume
- XYZ: "Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]" (search summary of Google formula; via https://www.joinleland.com/library/a/product-manager-resume-the-ultimate-guide-with-examples-and-template)
- Verbatim examples (Aced): "Cut mobile checkout drop-off 22% by shipping a one-tap flow with engineering and design"; "Reduced onboarding from 15 to 8 min via 20+ persona interviews across customer segments"; "Experimentation: Rebuilt the self-serve activation funnel and A/B tested three onboarding flows in Amplitude"
- Growth (https://resumeworded.com/growth-product-manager-resume-example, https://www.hrlens.io/articles/product-manager-resume-business-outcomes): "Raised new-user activation from 35% to 58% by A/B testing the onboarding flow across 15 experiments."; "Defined and ran the growth team's experimentation programme: 38 A/B tests in 12 months, 11 winners shipped, and +6.4% compounded conversion."; HRLens: name win rate, sample-size threshold, guardrail metrics.
- Data PM (https://huntr.co/resume-examples/product-manager, https://www.finalroundai.com/blog/product-manager-resume-examples, https://enhancv.com/resume-examples/data-product-manager/): reliability 97.2%->99.8% across 180 pipelines; freshness 6h->12min; self-serve analytics 2,300 MAU cut ad hoc requests 44%; Snowflake+dbt health-score product, forecast accuracy +18%.
- AI PM (https://stylingcv.com/blog/ai-product-manager-resume-2026-template-examples-tips-that-get-interviews/, https://cvcompiler.com/ai-product-manager-resume-examples): Action + AI component + business metric; evals (hallucination 12%->2.4%); quality/cost/latency tradeoff; RAG, F1, precision/recall, drift; 2-4 line summary with one proof point; 2-3 key projects Problem-Solution-Impact; only skills you can defend.

## Summary
- Aced: 3-5 lines: who, what, signature accomplishment, target role. Resume Worded: optional, 3-4 sentences, lead with quantified achievements, no objective statements, tailor. https://resumeworded.com/product-manager-resume-examples
- FR senior example accroche: "Product manager senior avec 10 ans sur des produits B2B et de place de marché, en charge de trois squads et de 22 M€ d'ARR... rétention nette de revenus de 94 % à 118 %" https://www.moncvhub.fr/blog/cv-chef-de-produit-product-manager-2026 (title line e.g. "Chef de Produit Senior | Spécialiste SaaS B2B")

## Layout / order
- EN order (Aced): contact, summary, experience, projects, skills, education (education lower for seniors). 1-2 pages beyond ~5 yrs. Single column, no photo/icons for ATS. Resume Worded: no photo/personal details for US.
- FR: photo optional (~10-15% of page, recent, plain background); avoid age/religion/marital status; centres d'intérêt optional, 2-3 specific items; 2 pages (3 max with 10+ yrs); PDF; expérience before formation. https://attractivecv.com/exemples-de-cv/cv-cadre/ https://recrute-talents.fr/infos-cv-2025/ https://modele-cv.com/conseils-cv/rediger-centres-dinterets/ https://www.hellowork.com/fr-fr/medias/cv-rubriques.html https://www.voilamoncv.fr/photo-cv
- APEC: Profil Apec requires Poste recherché, Souhaits, CV & Compétences; photo optional. https://www.apec.fr/files/live/sites/www/files/documents_reference/candidat/ProfilApecguide%20candidats_2024.pdf

## Consultant
- Employer = practice/"Self-Employed"; one entry with date range; lead with client type, industry, duration, ownership; bullets as mini case studies; repeat clients/retainers as proof; anonymise confidential clients. https://enhancv.com/resume-examples/freelance-consultant/ https://www.resumetemplates.com/consultant-resume-examples/

## AI-written CVs
- Resume Now 2025: 62% employers say uncustomised AI resumes more likely rejected; TopResume May 2025 (600 US HMs): 19.6% would reject, 33.5% spot in <20s; Resume.io Jan 2025: 49% dismiss suspected AI (single-source, high); Express/Harris Feb 2026: 86% say AI makes exaggeration easy. https://www.kraftcv.com/blog/ai-resumes-2026-what-hiring-managers-think https://resufit.com/blog/can-recruiters-tell-if-you-used-ai-to-write-your-resume/ https://www.zvee.io/en/resources/blog/is-ai-hurting-your-job-search
- Tells: synergize, leverage, dynamic, results-driven, spearhead, cutting-edge, passionate; vague accomplishments; identical bullet structure; no reference to the JD. Detectors unreliable (OpenAI detector 26%); recruiters spot generic, not AI.
- Common PM mistakes (Aced, Resume Worded): duty bullets ("Responsible for..."), bullets >2 lines, keywords without quantified backing, AI skills without hands-on evidence, multi-column/photo layouts, generic untailored CV, listing every cert.
- Lenny survey (not resume): business impact is #1 for promotion. https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/product-management-survey
