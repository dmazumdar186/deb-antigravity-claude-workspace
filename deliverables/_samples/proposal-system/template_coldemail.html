07 — What to Expect

The honest version

Cold email works, but it runs on infrastructure that other companies control. Here is everything I'd want to know if I were signing this, so nothing below is a surprise later.

  • You hear back within one business dayAny question, request or forwarded reply gets a response from me within one business day, and usually much sooner. Positive replies are handed to you as they land, not batched up for a weekly report.
  • Mailboxes wear outSending mailboxes gradually lose deliverability and will eventually start landing in spam. This is not a failure, it's how the channel works. Expect to replace them roughly every 2-3 months depending on volume. Replacement mailboxes and domains are a tool cost, billed to you like the rest of the stack.
  • The rules changeGoogle and Microsoft set the deliverability rules and change them without notice. The safe send limit per mailbox has already dropped from 30/day to 20/day, and may go lower. When it does, reaching the same volume needs more mailboxes, so tool costs rise accordingly. What works this month may need rebuilding next month.
  • Subscription prices moveVendor pricing is outside my control and tends to rise as we expand outreach. If we test a different strategy for better ROI, subscription spend can move up or down with it. I'll flag any change before it hits your card.

  • Results build over timeThe first campaign is a starting point, not a finished product. Copy, targeting, and list quality get sharpened based on what actually replies. The volume figures in this proposal are benchmarks, not guarantees.
