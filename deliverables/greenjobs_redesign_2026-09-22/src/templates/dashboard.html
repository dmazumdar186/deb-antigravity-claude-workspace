Event spec for launch Wired at launch

Nothing below is a live number. Each tile names the event the site sends (every apply link, search form, alert and newsletter form, "Request advertising rates" and "Post a job" button and save button already carries a data-ev attribute) and the target we will report against. Events are only sent once the visitor switches Analytics on in the cookie settings and a provider is connected; values fill in from launch day and a dash means "collected from launch".

Apply clicks per employer (30d)—

Event: apply_click with job_id, employer, sector, region and src_page; grouped by employer over a rolling thirty days. Use: the reach report each advertiser receives.

Apply clicks by referring network site—

Event: apply_click split by the referrer's network site, per posting. Use: shows each employer the distribution they paid for. Target: every posting records clicks from more than one network site (target we propose).

Job view to apply click—

Events: job_view then apply_click (the outbound link to the employer's listing). Target: at least 8% (target we propose; no public benchmark cited).

Search to job view—

Events: search then job_view in the same session. Target: at least 60% (target we propose).

Zero-result searches—

Event: search with results = 0, as a share of all searches. Use: each one is a missing sector, {{unit}} alias or landing page. Target: under 10% of searches (target we propose).

Alert match rate (weekly)—

Share of live alerts that matched at least one new role in the week, from the email provider's send log. Target: set after the first month's baseline.

Alert sign-ups—

Event: alert_signup. Target: set after the first month's baseline.

Newsletter sign-ups—

Event: newsletter_signup. Target: set after the first month's baseline.

Saved roles—

Event: save_role with job_id. Target: set after the first month's baseline.

Employer "Request advertising rates" clicks—

Event: request_rates on the home and Employers pages. Target: every click answered within one working day (target we propose).

"Post a job" clicks—

Event: post_job_click from the header, the home hero and the menu. Target: set after the first month's baseline.

Top search terms—

Event: search with the query as a property. Use: feeds the sector taxonomy and the landing pages. Target: every term searched 20+ times a month has a sector or landing page (target we propose).

Top landing pages—

Event: pageview, first page of the session. Use: which sector and {{unit}} pages earn organic entry. Target: landing and sector pages take a growing share of organic entries month on month (target we propose).

Organic share—

Referrer class of the session. Target: above half of sessions (target we propose).

Context, not targets: sessions and unique candidates (pageview, de-duplicated) are reported alongside so every rate above has a denominator.

Value to GreenJobs

Advertiser enquiries

What it is: employers asking for rates or starting a posting. How we measure it: request_rates and post_job_click events plus the enquiry inbox, reported monthly with the sector of the enquiry.

Network distribution reach

What it is: how far one posting travels across the network sites. How we measure it: sessions and apply clicks per posting, split by the referring network site, so each employer sees the reach they paid for.

Candidate database growth

What it is: candidates who leave a way to be reached. How we measure it: alert and newsletter sign-ups and saved roles, net of unsubscribes, reported as a monthly running total.

Health strip, reported monthly: Core Web Vitals field data (LCP within 2.5 s, INP within 200 ms, CLS within 0.1, the published "good" thresholds) and uptime from an external one-minute monitor (at least 99.9% a month, target we propose).
