Value to GreenJobs

Advertiser enquiries

What it is: employers asking for rates or starting a posting. How we measure it: request_rates and post_job_click events plus the enquiry inbox, reported monthly with the sector of the enquiry.

Network distribution reach

What it is: how far one posting travels across the network sites. How we measure it: sessions and apply clicks per posting, split by the referring network site, so each employer sees the reach they paid for.

Candidate database growth

What it is: candidates who leave a way to be reached. How we measure it: alert and newsletter sign-ups and saved roles, net of unsubscribes, reported as a monthly running total.
