## 02. What It Is Costing You

### Four problems on the current setup.

#### 1. Owning the platform

You have told me you are considering moving the boards to a platform you own, with hosting and integrations you control and changes delivered on your own timetable.

#### 2. Reaching candidates where they search

Job pages do not currently carry Google JobPosting structured data, so roles do not appear in Google for Jobs. Adding it, and showing salary wherever the employer provides one, extends the reach of every Premium posting.

#### 3. Speed on mobile

The current pages weigh about a megabyte per visit. The rebuilt boards serve the same content in under 200 KB with no third-party requests, which matters most to mobile visitors, the majority of job seekers.

#### 4. Live figures and a dashboard

Counts by county, sector and salary band are maintained by hand today. Your site manager asked whether they can update automatically; on the new platform every figure is computed from the job data on each post, with a KPI dashboard for you.

The following pages cover the full scope of the project we discussed, including investment. Let me know if you have any questions by emailing me at debanjan@prodcraft.fyi.

Debanjan

My proposed solution to the problems above is as follows.

#### 4. Numbers that update themselves

Every figure on the site is computed from the job data on every post, whether GreenJobs staff or a client uploads the ad.

## 04. Scope of Work

### What ProdCraft will build.

- Deploy the IE and UK editions to greenjobs.ie and greenjobs.co.uk on Cloudflare, including all 100 completed change-document items and the two site-manager fixes, JobPosting structured data on every role, sitemaps, and redirects for the current URLs.
- Build the platform: admin posting console, candidate accounts and job alerts, newsletter send, Broadbean / Idibu / LogicMelon posting endpoints with a daily test job, recruiter CRM posting integration, export and import of the current platform's data, 301 redirect map, analytics.
- Run the new platform in parallel with the current one for two weeks; move DNS only after every posting route passes and you sign off in writing.
- Hand over a written runbook, the test register (3,047 automated checks) and the source repository; everything runs in accounts GreenJobs owns.

#### Milestones

- **Week 1. Front end live.** The rebuilt boards are already built and tested (3,047 automated checks). Both editions go live on your domains within 5 working days of signature, once DNS access and logo permissions are in hand. If it is not live by then for reasons within my control, you do not pay for it.
- **Weeks 2 to 4. Platform build and staging.** Posting console, candidate accounts, alerts, Broadbean / Idibu / LogicMelon endpoints, recruiter CRM integration, data import, redirect map; staging acceptance with you and your site manager.
- **Weeks 5 to 6. Parallel run and go-live.** Two weeks alongside the current platform with a test job through every posting route each day, then DNS cut-over and 10 working days of daily checks.
- **Throughout.** Weekly update, one business day response, 4-hour response to a posting outage, all work in your accounts so nothing is locked to me.

My proposed timeline is pragmatic and takes into account past experience building similar systems. Assuming signature on Monday 6 October 2026:

**Out of scope:** employer self-service purchasing and card payment for job packages (GreenJobs staff post the ads); the other eight sites in the GreenJobs network; writing career-advice articles or market reports; paid advertising; any fees charged by Broadbean, Idibu, LogicMelon or the current platform provider.
