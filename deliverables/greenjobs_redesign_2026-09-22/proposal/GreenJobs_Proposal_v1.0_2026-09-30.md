## 02. What It Is Costing You

### Four problems on the current setup.

#### 1. Paying a vendor every month with no leverage

You pay Strategies a monthly fee for a hosted platform and wait 8 to 12 weeks for changes. You told me you are not convinced it is value for money and that you are considering moving off it.

#### 2. Invisible where candidates search

Google JobPosting structured data is switched off on every job page, so none of your roles appear in Google for Jobs. Salary shows on only a third of Irish roles, so Premium postings do not get the reach the package promises.

#### 3. A slow, heavy site on a legacy stack

Every visitor downloads about a megabyte of assets and a 190 KB page built on classic ASP, jQuery 1.11 and Internet Explorer 8 shims. Mobile visitors, the majority of job seekers, wait longest.

#### 4. Figures typed by hand, no dashboard

Counts by county, sector and salary band are typed in and go stale, and you have no employer analytics or KPI view. Your site manager's first question was whether the stats update automatically.

The following pages cover the full scope of the project we discussed, including investment. Let me know if you have any questions by emailing me at debanjan@prodcraft.fyi.

Thanks,

Debanjan

## 03. What You Get

### A modern GreenJobs platform you own, with the integrations kept.

My proposed solution to the problems above is as follows.

#### 1. Both editions rebuilt

greenjobs.ie and greenjobs.co.uk as demonstrated at greenjobs-redesign.pages.dev: jobs board with filters, list, map and saved views; one page per role with JobPosting structured data; 14-sector taxonomy; salary insights and salary guide; career quiz; employers page with the Premium and Membership comparison and a live post-a-job preview; four SEO landing pages; KPI dashboard. All 100 completed items from your change document are included; the remaining 11 are applied the day you supply the input.

#### 2. Your own platform, off Strategies

Admin posting console for GreenJobs staff; candidate accounts with stored CV, one-click apply, saved roles and email job alerts; Broadbean, Idibu and LogicMelon posting kept and verified by a daily test job; a recruiter CRM posting integration; full data export from Strategies; a 301 redirect for every indexed URL.

#### 3. Hosting that matches what Strategies gives you today

99.9% uptime, daily backups with a tested restore each quarter, firewall, DDoS protection and SSL, 24/7 monitoring, EU data region, daily integration checks and a monthly report.

#### 4. Numbers that update themselves

Every figure on the site is computed from the job data on every post, whether GreenJobs staff or a client uploads the ad.

I consider this reasonably straightforward and am confident I can do an outstanding job here for you. If I was not, I would not have put together this proposal.

## 04. Scope of Work

### What ProdCraft will build.

- Deploy the IE and UK editions to greenjobs.ie and greenjobs.co.uk on Cloudflare, including all 100 completed change-document items and the two site-manager fixes, JobPosting structured data on every role, sitemaps, and redirects for the current URLs.
- Build the platform: admin posting console, candidate accounts and job alerts, newsletter send, Broadbean / Idibu / LogicMelon posting endpoints with a daily test job, recruiter CRM posting integration, Strategies data export and import, 301 redirect map, analytics.
- Run the new platform in parallel with Strategies for 30 days; move DNS only after every posting route passes and you sign off in writing.
- Hand over a written runbook, the test register (3,047 automated checks) and the source repository; everything runs in accounts GreenJobs owns.

#### Milestones

- **Week 1. Front end live.** The rebuilt boards are already built and tested (3,047 automated checks). Both editions go live on your domains within 5 working days of signature, once DNS access and logo permissions are in hand. If it is not live by then for reasons within my control, you do not pay for it.
- **Weeks 2 to 4. Platform build and staging.** Posting console, candidate accounts, alerts, Broadbean / Idibu / LogicMelon endpoints, recruiter CRM integration, data import, redirect map; staging acceptance with you and your site manager.
- **Weeks 5 to 6. Parallel run and go-live.** Two weeks alongside Strategies with a test job through every posting route each day, then DNS cut-over and 10 working days of daily checks.
- **Throughout.** Weekly update, one business day response, 4-hour response to a posting outage, all work in your accounts so nothing is locked to me.

#### Timeline

My proposed timeline is pragmatic and takes into account past experience building similar systems. Assuming signature on Monday 6 October 2026:

- Front end live on greenjobs.ie and greenjobs.co.uk: by Monday 13 October 2026 (5 working days)
- Platform build and staging acceptance: 14 October to 3 November 2026
- Parallel run alongside Strategies: 4 to 17 November 2026
- Go-live on the new platform: Wednesday 18 November 2026

Dates move day for day with the signature date and with any input from GreenJobs that arrives late (Strategies contract details, which posting routes are in use, DNS access, logo permissions, testimonials, the open decisions from your change document). Broadbean onboarding runs on Broadbean's timeline.

**Out of scope:** employer self-service purchasing and card payment for job packages (GreenJobs staff post the ads); the other eight sites in the GreenJobs network; writing career-advice articles or market reports; paid advertising; any fees charged by Broadbean, Idibu, LogicMelon or Strategies. Anything else is quoted before it starts.
