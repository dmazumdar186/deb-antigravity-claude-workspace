Qualified Supervisor Electrician – Exeter

Are you an Electrician ready to be a true climate champion?

Naked Solar, the South West’s most beloved and rapidly growing solar company, is on the hunt for dedicated Electricians to join our dynamic team! If you’re passionate about making a real difference and want to work with cutting-edge renewable energy technology, then we want to hear from you.

This is your chance to step into a role where your skills directly contribute to a sustainable future, backed by a company that truly values its people. We offer an unparalleled benefits package and provide comprehensive training on industry-leading products like Tesla Powerwall, SolarEdge, MyEnergi, and many more.

Naked Solar is experiencing an exciting period of expansion, and we’re looking for individuals who are eager to grow with us and help shape the future of clean energy in the South West.

Benefits of joining the Naked Solar team include:

  • Monthly one-to-ones for professional development
  • Private Health Insurance
  • Cycle to Work Scheme
  • Travel Insurance
  • Company Pension

Role Overview

We are looking for a highly skilled Qualified Supervisor Electrician to join our Exeter Hub team. In this role, you will be responsible for the electrical aspects of installing solar PV systems and battery storage solutions, and ensuring all electrical installations are completed to the highest safety and quality standards, in line with NICEIC and BS7671 regulations.

Key Responsibilities

  • Act as the designated NICEIC Qualified Supervisor for the Exeter Hub.
  • Install, test, and commission solar PV and battery storage systems (domestic and commercial).
  • Ensure all electrical work is completed safely, compliantly, and in accordance with industry standards (BS7671, IET Wiring Regulations).
  • Supervise and sign off installation work carried out by any other electricians and apprentices.
  • Conduct site inspections, testing, and commissioning of solar PV and battery systems.
  • Maintain and manage certification, testing documentation, and compliance records.
  • Provide technical support and mentoring to future Exeter Hub electricians.
  • Maintain a strong focus on health & safety, conducting risk assessments where required.
  • Participate in audits, assessments, and technical reviews as needed.

Essential Skills & Experience

  • City & Guilds 2391 or 2394/2395 (Inspection & Testing).
  • NVQ Level 3 in Electrical Installation or equivalent.
  • Current 18th Edition Wiring Regulations (BS7671).
  • Experience installing domestic and/or commercial solar PV and battery storage systems.
  • NICEIC (or equivalent) Qualified Supervisor status (or eligible to become QS).
  • Strong knowledge of electrical safety and regulatory compliance.
  • Full UK driving licence.

Desirable

  • ECS Gold Card.
