Project Geotechnical Design Engineer

Location: Cork, Carlow or Dublin
Job Type: Permanent
Salary: Competitive

The Opportunity

An established multidisciplinary engineering consultancy is seeking a Project Geotechnical Design Engineer to join its growing Geotechnical team within the Sustainable Infrastructure group.

This role would suit a Civil Engineering graduate or Geology graduate with a Master’s in Geotechnical Engineering who is keen to develop their professional skills within a dynamic consultancy environment. You will contribute to a diverse portfolio of infrastructure projects across roads, greenways, active travel, renewable energy, water, waste and other major developments.

Key Responsibilities

  • Undertake geotechnical assessments, including peat stability and ground conditions.
  • Manage and supervise site investigations and associated field activities.
  • Carry out geotechnical design for roads, highways, active travel and greenways earthworks.
  • Design and assess slope stability, retaining structures and structural foundations.
  • Prepare and manage CAD documentation in collaboration with CAD technicians.
  • Work closely with engineers, scientists, graduates and other technical specialists.
  • Attend site visits, client meetings and multidisciplinary coordination meetings.
  • Support the delivery of projects within agreed timescales, budgets and quality standards.
  • Follow company quality, environmental, health and safety procedures and relevant legislation.

About You

  • Degree in Civil Engineering or Geology, with a Master’s in Geotechnical Engineering.
  • Experience in geotechnical engineering design, preferably within infrastructure projects.
  • Experience in a consulting engineering or similar professional environment.
  • Familiarity with AutoCAD and geotechnical design software.
  • Interested in progressing towards Chartered Engineer status.
  • Strong analytical, organisational, communication and project management skills.
  • Able to coordinate effectively with multidisciplinary teams and manage competing priorities.
  • Self-motivated, adaptable and comfortable working independently and as part of a team.
  • Willingness to travel to project sites and client meetings.
  • Full driving licence required.

This is an excellent opportunity for a motivated geotechnical engineer to build their experience on a varied portfolio of sustainable infrastructure projects while progressing their professional career.
