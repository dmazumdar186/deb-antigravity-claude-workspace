Solar Consultant – Newquay

Ready to help our customers make informed choices and clean up the planet within a B Corp Certified team?

Naked Solar are passionate about helping customers to really understand the choices of Solar PV & Battery Storage systems to ensure they make a fully informed decision about their investment.

Naked Solar need driven, energetic Solar Consultants who can show genuine care for the people they deal with, together with a detailed approach to gathering accurate information. Serving customers in various sectors including Homeowners, Property Developers, M&E Companies, Architects, and Self Builders. 

Working from our Newquay HQ you will be serving customers across the South West and your duties will include undertaking technical solar surveys online and supporting customers throughout the sales process to ensure they receive the best possible experience.

Full training will be provided; your personality and positive can-do attitude are the most important factors.

Essential:

  • Customer Service experience
  • Sales experience
  • Communication skills
  • Organisation and Planning skills
  • Attention to detail
  • Full UK Driving Licence
  • IT Literate

Desirable:

  • A passion for renewable energy
  • Technical sales experience
  • Salary will be depending on experience and performance:  Starting at £28,000 up to Level 7 Consultant earning £52,000.
