Environmental Engineer – Major Infrastructure

Location: Dublin, Ireland
Job Type: Permanent
Sector: Rail & Major Infrastructure

Read the full descriptionShow less

The Opportunity

A leading multidisciplinary consultancy is seeking an experienced Environmental Engineer to support the environmental performance of a major rail infrastructure programme in Dublin.

Working as part of a multidisciplinary project team, you will provide environmental oversight and assurance across a defined section of civil works, helping to ensure project commitments, planning requirements and environmental standards are effectively embedded throughout design and construction.

Key Responsibilities

  • Monitor compliance with environmental commitments, planning conditions and project requirements.
  • Review contractor environmental management plans, design submissions and supporting documentation.
  • Provide environmental input during design development and assist in developing practical mitigation solutions.
  • Act as the primary environmental contact for an assigned section of works.
  • Work closely with contractors, designers, engineers and other project stakeholders.
  • Support stakeholder and third-party engagement alongside the wider project team.
  • Monitor contractor performance against environmental management plans and project standards.
  • Review environmental monitoring data, mitigation measures and performance information.
  • Identify environmental risks and support the development and implementation of corrective actions.
  • Contribute to project reporting, planning, risk management and quality assurance processes.
  • Escalate significant environmental issues and provide technical advice to project leadership.

About You

  • Degree in Environmental Science, Environmental Engineering, Geoscience or a related discipline.
  • Significant experience in environmental management on major infrastructure or rail projects.
  • Strong understanding of Environmental Impact Assessment and Irish planning legislation.
  • Experience working within multidisciplinary project environments.
  • Strong environmental risk management and technical assurance skills.
  • Excellent communication, stakeholder engagement and problem-solving abilities.
  • Highly organised with strong analytical and reporting skills.

Desirable

  • Experience on major rail, metro or underground infrastructure programmes.
  • Membership of a relevant professional body such as IEMA, ISEP, CIWEM, CIWM or IEI.
  • Experience with contractor oversight, project controls and environmental reporting.
  • Knowledge of BS, EN and IS environmental specifications and standards.

This is an opportunity to contribute to a landmark infrastructure programme while playing a key role in environmental compliance, risk management and sustainable project delivery.
