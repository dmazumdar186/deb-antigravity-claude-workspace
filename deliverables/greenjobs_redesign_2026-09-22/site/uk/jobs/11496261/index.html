Senior Manager of EIA

Location: London, Greater London, United Kingdom

Capabilities: Environmental

Office Setup: Hybrid

At Jacobs, we're challenging today to reinvent tomorrow by solving the world's most critical problems for thriving cities, resilient environments, mission-critical outcomes, operational advancement, scientific discovery and cutting-edge manufacturing, turning abstract ideas into realities that transform the world for good.

Your impact

Help shape the future of some of the UK's most important infrastructure and environmental projects.

As a Senior Manager within our Environmental Planning team, you'll lead environmental assessment and consenting strategies that enable major projects to move from concept to reality. From renewable energy and hydrogen schemes to water, transport and nationally significant infrastructure projects, your expertise will directly influence sustainable outcomes for communities, clients and the environment.

Working with leading organisations including National Grid, Environment Agency, Water Utilities, National Highways, Network Rail and HS2, you'll bring together multidisciplinary teams, build trusted client relationships and help deliver projects that make a lasting positive impact.

At Jacobs, you'll have the opportunity to do meaningful work, collaborate with industry-leading experts and shape your career in a business that genuinely invests in its people.

About the Opportunity

Our Water & Environment business is recognised as a leader in environmental consultancy, delivering innovative solutions across the UK and Europe.

We're looking for a passionate and experienced EIA professional to join our growing Environmental Planning team. In this role, you'll lead environmental assessment and coordination activities for major infrastructure projects, working closely with clients, regulators and technical specialists to drive successful outcomes.

You'll be part of a friendly, highly regarded team delivering projects across sectors including:

  • Renewable energy and hydrogen
  • Water and water resources
  • Transport and rail
  • Utilities and transmission networks
  • Major infrastructure and development

This is an opportunity to step into a senior leadership role, influence strategic decision-making and help grow a talented team that is passionate about creating a more sustainable future.

Here's what you'll need

  • A degree in a relevant environmental, planning or scientific discipline
  • Significant experience delivering Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) and environmental coordination for infrastructure projects
  • Strong client-facing and stakeholder engagement experience
  • Proven leadership and project management capability
  • Chartered status and membership of a relevant professional body such as ISEP, CIWEM or IES, or a commitment to achieve this with our support

Why Jacobs?

The opportunity to lead high-profile, nationally significant projects

  • A collaborative, inclusive culture where your ideas and expertise are valued

At Jacobs, we're challenging today to reinvent tomorrow. We believe diversity fuels innovation, inclusion drives success, and great careers are built when people are empowered to be themselves.

If you're ready to influence major projects, grow your leadership career and help create a more sustainable future, we'd love to hear from you.
