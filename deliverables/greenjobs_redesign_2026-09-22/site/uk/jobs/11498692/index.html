Arborist

Gaia Talent
CorkPermanentup to €50k

An established independent biodiversity and sustainability consultancy is looking to add an experienced Arborist to its team. This is a new in-house capability that will complement an existing ecology offering and strengthen the consultancy’s ability to provide joined-up arboricultural and
ecological advice to clients across Ireland.
The position is suited to someone who combines a strong practical arboricultural skillset with the ability to deliver clear, technically robust survey outputs and written reports to a high professional standard.

Key Responsibilities:
  • Undertake arboricultural field surveys and assessments for a varied portfolio of development and environmental projects.
  • Prepare high-quality arboricultural reports, survey outputs and practical recommendations for clients and project teams.
  • Provide clear arboricultural advice throughout planning and project delivery, identifying tree-related constraints, risks and opportunities.
  • Work closely with ecologists and other environmental professionals to provide coordinated arboricultural and ecological input.
  • Liaise professionally with clients and project stakeholders, communicating technical findings in a practical and accessible way.
  • Support the development of arboriculture as a complementary discipline within the wider consultancy service offering.
  • Contribute to consistent, technically sound and commercially focused project delivery across public and private sector work.
Experience & Profile:
  • A minimum of 5 years’ professional arboricultural experience.
  • Strong practical experience in arboricultural survey and assessment work.
  • Proven report-writing capability, with the ability to produce clear, accurate and high-quality technical documentation.
  • Sound professional judgement and confidence working independently on arboricultural assignments.
  • Strong communication skills and a client-focused approach.
  • Comfortable collaborating with ecology and environmental consultancy teams on multidisciplinary projects.
  • A relevant arboricultural, forestry, environmental or related qualification would be advantageous.
Project Environment:

The consultancy operates nationwide and supports public and private sector clients across areas including conservation, renewable energy, industrial development, transport and residential development. Its ecology work spans pre-planning survey and assessment, planning and construction compliance, and post- construction monitoring.

Why Consider This Opportunity?
  • Opportunity to become the in-house arboricultural specialist within a growing environmental consultancy.
  • A varied role combining practical fieldwork, technical reporting and direct client input.
  • Close collaboration with an established ecology team and involvement in multidisciplinary environmental projects.
  • Four-day working week.
  • Salary up to €50,000, depending on experience.
