How to read these figures

  • Only roles that publish a figure are counted; "competitive" and "depending on experience" are not guessed at.
  • 1 of the disclosed salaries is advertised in another currency; for the figures on this page they are converted at a fixed rate of 1 GBP = 1.17 EUR. On role cards and job pages every salary stays in the currency the employer advertised.
  • Compare disclosed salaries and identify employers committed to greater pay transparency: the salary explorer shows the same data as charts, and the jobs search can be limited to roles that disclose.
