Principal Engineer – Transportation

Gaia Talent
Dublin, LouthPermanent

Principal Engineer – Transportation

Location: Dublin or Dundalk, Ireland
Job Type: Full-time
Sector: Transportation & Infrastructure

About the Role

A leading multidisciplinary engineering consultancy is seeking an experienced Principal Engineer – Transportation to join its growing Transportation team.

This is an excellent opportunity for a Chartered Engineer with strong experience in the planning and design of transport infrastructure projects. You will take a leading role in the delivery of road, rail, public transport and active travel schemes across Ireland.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead the planning, design and delivery of transportation projects.
  • Manage multidisciplinary design teams and project resources.
  • Work across feasibility, preliminary and detailed design stages.
  • Provide input into statutory processes, including EIAR and CPO requirements.
  • Prepare contract documents and support procurement processes.
  • Assist with contract administration and construction supervision.
  • Support commercial management and project delivery.
  • Ensure designs meet relevant standards and provide practical, buildable and cost-effective solutions.
  • Develop strong relationships with clients and project stakeholders.
  • Prepare and lead tender proposals and contribute to winning new commissions.
  • Coordinate with internal teams, contractors and external consultants.
  • Take responsibility for project quality, programme and technical delivery.

Requirements

  • Degree in Engineering (Level 8 or 9) or a related discipline.
  • Chartered Engineer status.
  • Relevant consultancy experience within transportation engineering.
  • Strong experience in the planning and design of transport infrastructure.
  • Experience delivering projects in Ireland is highly desirable.
  • Knowledge of road, rail, public transport or active travel schemes.
  • Experience across feasibility, preliminary and detailed design.
  • Understanding of statutory approvals, procurement and contract processes.
  • Proven experience managing multidisciplinary teams and projects.
  • Strong commercial awareness and project management skills.
  • Experience preparing tender proposals and leading bids.
  • Excellent written, verbal and interpersonal communication skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities and work effectively under pressure.
  • Self-motivated, flexible and comfortable working independently or within a team.

What’s on Offer

  • Senior technical leadership role on major Irish transport projects.
  • Exposure to road, rail, public transport and active travel schemes.
  • Opportunities to develop client relationships and business development skills.
  • Professional training and career development.
  • Supportive multidisciplinary working environment.
  • Competitive salary and comprehensive benefits package.
