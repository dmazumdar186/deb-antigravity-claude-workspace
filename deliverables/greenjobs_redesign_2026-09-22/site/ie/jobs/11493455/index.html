Resident Engineer – Bridge Construction

Location: Galway/Mayo Region, Ireland
Job Type: Full-time, Fixed-term (12 months)
Sector: Civil & Structural Engineering

About the Role

A leading engineering consultancy is seeking an experienced Resident Engineer to join a site supervision team on a major bridge construction, rehabilitation and maintenance project in the Galway/Mayo region.

This is an excellent opportunity for a motivated civil or structural engineer with strong bridge experience to take responsibility for site-based supervision, inspection and contract administration.

Key Responsibilities

  • Supervise and monitor bridge construction and rehabilitation works on site.
  • Inspect construction activities to ensure compliance with designs, specifications and contract requirements.
  • Monitor contractor progress, quality and programme.
  • Maintain accurate site records, reports and project documentation.
  • Administer construction activities and support contract management.
  • Record and report on contractor performance and project progress.
  • Liaise with contractors, designers, clients and other project stakeholders.
  • Ensure construction works comply with relevant Health & Safety requirements.
  • Support the wider site supervision team in achieving project objectives.
  • Identify construction issues and support appropriate solutions.
  • Attend site meetings and provide technical input where required.

About You

  • Degree in Civil or Structural Engineering.
  • Relevant postgraduate experience as a Resident Engineer or similar site-based role.
  • Strong experience in the construction, repair or rehabilitation of masonry, concrete and steel bridge structures.
  • Experience with site inspection, supervision and contractor reporting.
  • Knowledge of current Health & Safety regulations and construction best practice.
  • Experience administering Public Works contracts would be advantageous.
  • Proficiency in AutoCAD and Microsoft Office.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Strong organisational and record-keeping abilities.
  • Ability to work independently and effectively as part of a multidisciplinary team.
  • Full driving licence and valid Safe Pass.

What’s on Offer

  • Opportunity to work on a significant bridge infrastructure project.
  • Hands-on site-based engineering experience.
  • Exposure to construction, rehabilitation and contract administration.
  • Supportive professional environment.
  • Competitive salary and benefits package.
  • Opportunity to further develop your civil and structural engineering career.
