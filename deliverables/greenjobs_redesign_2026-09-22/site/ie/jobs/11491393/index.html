Resident Engineer

Location: Dublin, Ireland
Job Type: Full-time
Contract: Approximately 6 months, with potential for future opportunities

Overview

We are seeking an experienced Resident Engineer to support an Active Travel scheme in County Meath, commencing July/August. This is an excellent opportunity for a motivated Civil Engineer with experience in roads and transport infrastructure to take a key role in the successful delivery of a major construction project.

The successful candidate will be self-motivated, enthusiastic and flexible, with the ability to take responsibility and work effectively both independently and as part of a wider project team.

Key Responsibilities

The role will involve:

  • Site-based supervision of construction activities.
  • Contract administration and monitoring.
  • Stakeholder and client liaison.
  • Regular reporting and progress monitoring.
  • Inspection of construction works to ensure compliance with project requirements.
  • Working closely with the Senior Resident Engineer.
  • Monitoring contractor performance, progress and quality.
  • Maintaining accurate project records and documentation.
  • Supporting the administration of Public Works Contracts, including valuations and claims.

Requirements

  • Level 8 Degree in Civil Engineering or a related discipline.
  • Chartered Engineer status or working towards chartership is desirable.
  • Minimum 4 years’ relevant civil engineering experience, including at least 2 years’ construction-phase experience supervising and administering Public Works Contracts.
  • Experience supervising roadworks, including pavement, drainage, structures, ducting, signage and lighting.
  • Experience administering Public Works Contracts, including valuations and claims.
  • Proficiency with AutoCAD or similar software and Microsoft Office.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills.
  • Good record-keeping and reporting abilities.
  • Knowledge of current health and safety regulations and best practices.
  • Full driving licence and valid Safe Pass.
  • Personal Qualities
The ideal candidate will demonstrate excellent interpersonal and communication skills, a positive and approachable manner, strong organisational abilities and the flexibility to manage multiple priorities. You should be comfortable working independently while also contributing effectively to a multidisciplinary team.

Benefits

Competitive salary, professional development opportunities, training, health and wellbeing benefits, flexible working arrangements and opportunities for future career progression.
