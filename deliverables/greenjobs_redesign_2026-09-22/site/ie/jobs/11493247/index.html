Associate Director – Civil & Environmental Engineering

Associate Director – Civil & Environmental Engineering

Location: Cork, Dublin or Carlow
Sector: Circular Economy & Environmental Engineering

Read the full descriptionShow less

About the Role

A leading Irish multidisciplinary consultancy is seeking an experienced Associate Director – Civil & Environmental Engineering to join its growing Circular Economy and Environmental team.

This senior leadership opportunity is suited to an experienced professional with 15+ years’ industry experience, offering involvement across a diverse portfolio of major environmental and circular economy infrastructure projects. These may include waste treatment facilities, recycling facilities, waste transfer stations, energy-from-waste projects, landfill remediation, landfill engineering and public realm developments.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead civil and environmental design teams, ensuring projects meet programme, budget and quality requirements.
  • Manage multidisciplinary project delivery and coordinate technical outputs.
  • Develop new business opportunities and strengthen client relationships.
  • Mentor and support senior and junior engineering professionals.
  • Prepare and review technical reports, designs and tender documentation.
  • Plan and manage design workflows in collaboration with Project Owners and technical leads.
  • Oversee project budgets, financial performance and commercial delivery.
  • Ensure compliance with quality management systems and Health & Safety requirements.
  • Coordinate clients, subconsultants and multidisciplinary project teams.
  • Attend site visits, client meetings and stakeholder engagements.
  • Contribute to business development and strategic growth activities.
  • Support planning processes and, where required, present at Oral Hearings.

Requirements

  • Level 8 or Level 9 qualification in Civil Engineering, Environmental Engineering or a related discipline.
  • Chartered Engineer status.
  • Minimum 15+ years’ professional experience in engineering design and project leadership.
  • Proven multidisciplinary design management experience.
  • Strong client, stakeholder and subconsultant management skills.
  • Demonstrated experience in business development and winning new work.
  • Strong financial and budget management capabilities.
  • Excellent communication, analytical and leadership skills.
  • Self-motivated with strong problem-solving abilities.
  • Full clean driving licence and willingness to travel.

What’s on Offer

  • Senior leadership position with significant project responsibility.
  • Exposure to major circular economy and environmental infrastructure projects.
  • Clear pathway towards Director-level progression.
  • Opportunities to influence business growth and team development.
  • Collaborative multidisciplinary environment.
  • Competitive salary and benefits package.
