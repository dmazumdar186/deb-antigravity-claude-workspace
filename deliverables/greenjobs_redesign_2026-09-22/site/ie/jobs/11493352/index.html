Principal Project Manager – Sustainable Infrastructure

Location: Cork, Carlow or Dublin
Sector: Sustainable Infrastructure

About the Role

A leading multidisciplinary engineering consultancy is seeking an experienced Principal Project Manager to join its Sustainable Infrastructure team.

This is an excellent opportunity for a Chartered Civil Engineer with 12+ years’ postgraduate experience to take a senior role in the delivery of major infrastructure projects across Ireland. The portfolio includes national roads, greenways, active travel, public transport, renewable energy and Design & Build projects.

The successful candidate will lead multidisciplinary teams, manage client relationships and oversee projects from design through to delivery, with clear opportunities for progression into Associate and Director-level positions.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead multidisciplinary design teams to deliver projects on time, within budget and to required quality standards.
  • Manage project programmes, resources and technical deliverables.
  • Supervise, mentor and develop senior and junior engineering staff.
  • Prepare and review technical reports, specifications and tender documentation.
  • Plan and coordinate design activities with Engineering Discipline Leads and Technicians.
  • Manage client relationships and project communications.
  • Coordinate with clients, stakeholders, subconsultants and multidisciplinary teams.
  • Ensure compliance with quality management systems and Health & Safety legislation.
  • Monitor project budgets, resources and delivery requirements.
  • Support the successful delivery of Design & Build projects.
  • Attend site visits, client meetings and stakeholder engagements.
  • Contribute to business development and project proposals where required.

Requirements

  • Degree in Civil Engineering or a related discipline.
  • Chartered Engineer status.
  • Minimum 12 years’ postgraduate experience in infrastructure design and project management.
  • Proven multidisciplinary design management experience.
  • Experience delivering infrastructure projects in Ireland.
  • Highway or greenway experience to TII and NTA standards is advantageous.
  • Design & Build experience would be beneficial.
  • Strong client, stakeholder and subconsultant management skills.
  • Experience creating and managing project budgets.
  • Excellent communication, organisational and analytical skills.
  • Strong leadership and problem-solving abilities.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively.
  • Full driving licence and willingness to travel.

What’s on Offer

  • Senior leadership role on major infrastructure projects.
  • Diverse portfolio across transport, renewables and sustainable infrastructure.
  • Clear career progression towards Associate and Director positions.
  • Strong client and stakeholder exposure.
  • Supportive multidisciplinary environment.
  • Competitive salary and benefits package.
