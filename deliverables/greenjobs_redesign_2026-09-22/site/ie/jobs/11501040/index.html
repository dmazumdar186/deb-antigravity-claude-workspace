Senior Engineer – Transmission & Distribution

Location: Dublin, Ireland
Job Type: Permanent
Sector: Energy, Power & Utilities

The Opportunity

A leading engineering consultancy is seeking an experienced Senior Engineer – Transmission & Distribution to provide technical leadership across major high-voltage transmission projects.

This role offers the opportunity to support the development, review, construction, pre-commissioning and commissioning of critical electricity infrastructure, acting as a key technical interface between project teams, contractors and clients. The successful candidate will provide engineering assurance across a range of transmission assets, including substations, overhead lines and cable systems.

Key Responsibilities

  • Review detailed engineering designs to ensure compliance with functional requirements, client specifications and national and international standards.
  • Assess and review technical and functional specifications for transmission infrastructure projects.
  • Manage technical queries throughout the project lifecycle, ensuring issues are resolved efficiently and appropriately documented.
  • Provide technical guidance and challenge designs where required to maintain high engineering standards.
  • Undertake site inspections and monitor construction activities to confirm works are delivered in accordance with approved designs.
  • Carry out pre-commissioning and commissioning inspections to verify system readiness and compliance.
  • Participate in project reviews, technical audits, factory acceptance tests and approval processes.
  • Liaise with clients, contractors and multidisciplinary teams to support successful project delivery.
  • Mentor junior engineers and contribute to the development of technical capability within the team.

About You

  • Degree qualified in Electrical Engineering or a related discipline.
  • Strong technical knowledge of transmission infrastructure, particularly substations operating at 110kV, 220kV and 400kV.
  • Experience across substations, overhead lines and high-voltage cable systems.
  • Ability to assess designs, construction activities and commissioning processes against technical, operational and safety requirements.
  • Understanding of key design deliverables, including single line diagrams, protection schemes, interlocking systems and substation layouts.
  • Experience carrying out site inspections, construction monitoring and commissioning activities.
  • Excellent communication, report writing and stakeholder management skills.
  • Strong organisational abilities with a proactive and collaborative approach.
  • Experience mentoring engineers or providing technical leadership is desirable.

This is an excellent opportunity to play a key role in the delivery of major transmission and grid infrastructure projects while contributing to the development of a more resilient and sustainable energy network.
