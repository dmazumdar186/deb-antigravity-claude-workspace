Project Hydrogeologist – Environment & Planning

Location: Dublin, Galway, Limerick, Sligo or Castlebar
Sector: Environment & Planning

About the Role

A leading multidisciplinary environmental and engineering consultancy is seeking an experienced Project Hydrogeologist to join its growing Environment & Planning team.

This is an excellent opportunity for a hydrogeologist with 4–10 years’ experience to develop their technical expertise while working on projects across infrastructure, renewable energy, transport, water and environmental sectors.

Key Responsibilities

  • Deliver hydrogeological and environmental projects from inception through to completion.
  • Undertake fieldwork, site investigations, groundwater monitoring and environmental surveys.
  • Prepare and review technical reports, groundwater assessments and environmental documentation.
  • Coordinate specialist surveys, site investigations and monitoring programmes.
  • Liaise with clients, landowners, contractors, regulators and other stakeholders.
  • Contribute to EIARs, PECRs, Appropriate Assessment Screenings and NISs.
  • Review maps, GIS outputs and technical reports prepared by junior team members.
  • Support peer reviews and liaise with legal, regulatory and project teams.
  • Attend stakeholder meetings, public consultations and community engagement events.
  • Assist with tenders, proposals and business development activities.
  • Work closely with Project Managers and senior technical specialists.
  • Maintain CPD records and actively pursue professional development.

Requirements

  • Level 8 Honours Degree in Hydrogeology, Geology, Environmental Science, Earth Sciences or a related discipline.
  • Approximately 4–10 years’ relevant experience in hydrogeology or environmental consultancy.
  • PGeo, ACIEEM or equivalent professional accreditation is desirable.
  • Experience in hydrogeological investigations and environmental assessments.
  • Knowledge of borehole specification, groundwater monitoring and trial pit logging.
  • Strong fieldwork and surveying capabilities.
  • Proficiency in ArcGIS, MapInfo or equivalent GIS platforms.
  • Strong Microsoft Office skills, including Word and Excel.
  • Full clean driving licence and willingness to undertake fieldwork.

Desirable

Experience with AquiferTest, MODFLOW, LandSim, P20 or similar hydrogeological software would be advantageous.

What’s on Offer

  • Varied environmental and infrastructure projects.
  • Supportive multidisciplinary working environment.
  • Technical and professional development opportunities.
  • Career progression and exposure to major projects across Ireland.
