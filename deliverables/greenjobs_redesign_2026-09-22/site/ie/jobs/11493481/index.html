Graduate LVIA Specialist

Location: Galway, Ireland
Job Type: Full-time, Permanent
Sector: Landscape, Planning & Environment

About the Role

A leading planning and environmental consultancy is seeking a Graduate LVIA Specialist to join its growing team in Galway.

This is an excellent opportunity for a creative graduate interested in Landscape and Visual Impact Assessment (LVIA) and environmental planning. You will contribute to a diverse range of infrastructure projects, including renewable energy, grid infrastructure, extraction and residential developments.

Key Responsibilities

  • Support the preparation of Landscape and Visual Impact Assessments (LVIAs).
  • Undertake fieldwork and photography to support landscape and visual assessments.
  • Prepare baseline constraints mapping and spatial analysis.
  • Produce high-quality maps and visual outputs using GIS and related tools.
  • Assist with reports for planning applications and Environmental Impact Assessment Reports (EIARs).
  • Research landscape, environmental and planning information.
  • Work closely with graphics specialists and multidisciplinary project teams.
  • Coordinate assessment information and ensure project outputs are delivered accurately and on time.
  • Contribute to projects across renewable energy, grid infrastructure, extraction and housing.
  • Develop your technical knowledge through mentoring and practical project experience.

About You

  • Degree in Landscape Planning, Landscape Architecture, Planning, Geography, Environmental Science or a related discipline.
  • Strong interest in landscape, visual assessment and environmental planning.
  • Excellent report writing and presentation skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and ability to produce accurate work.
  • Creative, confident and enthusiastic approach.
  • Good organisational and communication skills.
  • Ability to work collaboratively within a multidisciplinary team.
  • Willingness to learn and develop professionally.

Desirable Skills

  • Experience using GIS software.
  • Understanding of the Irish planning system and planning policy.
  • Knowledge of environmental policy and assessment processes.
  • Experience with photography and surveying techniques used for photomontages.
  • Valid driving licence.

What’s on Offer

  • Full-time permanent position in Galway.
  • Hands-on experience across major infrastructure projects.
  • Training and mentoring from experienced professionals.
  • Opportunity to develop specialist LVIA and GIS skills.
  • Clear opportunities for professional growth and career progression.
  • Collaborative and supportive working environment.
  • Competitive salary and benefits package.
