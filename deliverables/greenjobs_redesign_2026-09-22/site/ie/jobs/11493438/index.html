Consents Manager

Sector: Rail Infrastructure & Programme Management

Read the full descriptionShow less

About the Role

A leading infrastructure consultancy is seeking an experienced Consents Manager to lead the strategy, coordination and delivery of statutory and regulatory consents for a major rail infrastructure programme.

You will act as the central point of authority for consent-related matters, ensuring approvals are secured on schedule, compliance requirements are managed effectively and potential programme risks are identified and addressed.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and maintain an integrated Consents Strategy aligned with programme milestones.
  • Lead planning and consent activities under Railway Order or equivalent statutory approval processes.
  • Maintain a comprehensive Consents Schedule covering all required permissions and approvals.
  • Track and manage consent conditions, commitments and obligations.
  • Embed consent requirements into design, environmental assessment, procurement and construction activities.
  • Support project teams and contractors with the discharge of planning conditions.
  • Develop and manage Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs) with local authorities and statutory bodies.
  • Build strong relationships with planning authorities, regulators, statutory consultees and stakeholders.
  • Act as the primary interface with consenting authorities.
  • Support public and stakeholder engagement activities.
  • Coordinate with property and land teams on acquisition, safeguarding and access matters.
  • Maintain a Consents Risk Register and develop appropriate mitigation measures.
  • Provide regular reporting on consent status, risks and issues to programme leadership.
  • Manage external consultants, advisors and specialist technical inputs.
  • Support audits and ensure compliance with statutory and programme requirements.

Requirements

  • Relevant degree or professional qualification in Planning, Environmental Management, Engineering, Law or a related discipline.
  • Significant experience managing statutory consents and approvals on major infrastructure projects.
  • Strong understanding of Irish planning and regulatory processes.
  • Experience with major rail, transport or infrastructure programmes is highly desirable.
  • Excellent stakeholder and authority engagement skills.
  • Strong project, programme and risk management capabilities.
  • Experience coordinating multidisciplinary teams and external consultants.
  • Excellent communication, reporting and organisational skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple consent processes and competing deadlines.
  • Strong attention to detail and understanding of compliance requirements.

What’s on Offer

  • Senior role on a major infrastructure programme.
  • Opportunity to influence complex statutory approval processes.
  • Extensive stakeholder and regulatory exposure.
  • Professional development and career progression.
  • Collaborative multidisciplinary environment.
  • Competitive salary and comprehensive benefits package.
