Associate – Renewables

Gaia Talent
Carlow, Dublin, CorkPermanent

Senior / Principal Engineer – Renewables

Location: Cork, Carlow or Dublin
Sector: Renewable Energy & Sustainable Infrastructure

Read the full descriptionShow less

About the Role

A leading multidisciplinary engineering consultancy is seeking an experienced Senior or Principal Engineer – Renewables to join its Sustainable Infrastructure team.

This is an excellent opportunity to work on a diverse portfolio of renewable energy projects, including wind farms, solar farms and battery energy storage systems (BESS). You will contribute to projects across design, independent checking, Design & Build and Owner’s Engineer services.

The role offers strong opportunities for professional development and progression towards Associate and Director-level positions.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead or contribute to multidisciplinary design teams.
  • Ensure projects are delivered on time, within budget and to required quality standards.
  • Prepare engineering designs, technical reports and tender documentation.
  • Plan and coordinate design activities with Engineering Discipline Leads and Technicians.
  • Supervise, mentor and support junior and senior engineering staff.
  • Coordinate with multidisciplinary project teams and technical specialists.
  • Liaise with clients, contractors, stakeholders and subconsultants.
  • Ensure compliance with quality management systems, Health & Safety requirements and relevant legislation.
  • Support project delivery from design through to construction.
  • Attend site visits, client meetings and stakeholder engagements.
  • Contribute to the continued development of renewable energy services.

Requirements

  • Degree in Civil Engineering or a related discipline.
  • Chartered Engineer status.
  • Minimum 8 years’ postgraduate experience for Senior level or 12 years for Principal level.
  • Strong experience in infrastructure design and project delivery in Ireland or the UK.
  • Experience in renewable energy projects is advantageous.
  • Design & Build experience would be beneficial.
  • Proven multidisciplinary design management experience.
  • Strong client, stakeholder and subconsultant engagement skills.
  • Excellent communication, organisational and analytical abilities.
  • Flexible, collaborative and able to work effectively within a team.
  • Self-motivated with the ability to work independently.
  • Full driving licence and willingness to travel.

What’s on Offer

  • Opportunity to work on major renewable energy projects.
  • Exposure to wind, solar and BESS developments.
  • Senior technical and project responsibilities.
  • Strong career progression towards Associate and Director positions.
  • Supportive multidisciplinary environment.
  • Competitive salary and benefits package.
