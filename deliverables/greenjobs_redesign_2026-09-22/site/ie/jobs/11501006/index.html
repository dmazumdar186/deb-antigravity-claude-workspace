Associate / Associate Director – Metrolink

Location: Dublin, Ireland
Job Type: Permanent
Sector: Roads, Transportation & Infrastructure

The Opportunity

A leading multidisciplinary engineering consultancy is seeking an experienced Associate / Associate Director to join its growing Roads and Transportation team in Dublin.

This is a senior leadership opportunity for a Chartered Civil Engineer or Incorporated Engineer with extensive post-professional qualification experience in the design and project management of major road and public transport schemes. The successful candidate will play a key role in delivering landmark infrastructure projects, including major public transport and active travel programmes.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead the technical delivery and project management of medium to high-complexity transport and infrastructure schemes.
  • Manage multidisciplinary design teams, resources, programmes and budgets.
  • Lead preliminary and detailed design from project inception through to completion and client handover.
  • Coordinate client requirements, technical queries, RFIs, design changes and engineering assurance.
  • Ensure designs meet programme, budget, quality, health and safety and technical requirements.
  • Provide technical review, mentoring and guidance to engineers and design teams.
  • Manage specialist subconsultants and multidisciplinary inputs.
  • Agree project fees and monitor commercial performance.
  • Contribute to tender submissions, business development and client relationship management.
  • Develop strong relationships across the wider organisation to support multidisciplinary project delivery.

About You

  • Chartered Civil Engineer or Incorporated Engineer.
  • Extensive post-professional qualification experience in roads, transport or infrastructure.
  • Proven experience leading the delivery of medium to high-complexity projects.
  • Strong understanding of TII, NTA and Local Authority procedures and project requirements.
  • Knowledge of DMURS, Traffic Signs Manual, National Cycle Manual and TII design standards.
  • Experience with transport design, maintenance and supervision.
  • Familiarity with Civil 3D, OpenRoads, MX or WinDes is desirable.
  • Strong project management, communication and client-facing skills.
  • Excellent IT skills, including MS Project.

This is an opportunity to take a senior leadership role on major transport infrastructure programmes while contributing to team growth, technical excellence and business development.
