Civil Engineering Technician

Location: Dublin, Ireland
Job Type: Full-time
Sector: Civil Engineering & Infrastructure

About the Role

A leading multidisciplinary engineering consultancy is seeking Civil Engineering Technicians at junior and mid-career levels to join its growing team in Dublin.

This is an excellent opportunity to work on a varied portfolio of projects, including industrial, commercial and residential developments, as well as aviation infrastructure. You will work alongside experienced engineers and technicians within a collaborative multidisciplinary environment.

Key Responsibilities

  • Prepare accurate 2D drawings and 3D models for civil infrastructure projects.
  • Produce designs for roads, drainage and utility infrastructure.
  • Use AutoCAD and Civil 3D for drawing and modelling.
  • Undertake hydraulic and SuDS design using appropriate software such as Causeway Flow.
  • Assist with design reviews, technical detailing and drawing production.
  • Support multidisciplinary design coordination and quality control.
  • Ensure drawings and designs comply with relevant standards and specifications.
  • Maintain accurate project documentation and technical records.
  • Provide technical guidance and support to junior technicians where appropriate.
  • Contribute to the successful delivery of projects within agreed programmes and quality requirements.

About You

The ideal candidate will have:

  • Relevant qualification in Civil Engineering, Engineering Technology or a related discipline.
  • Experience working within a civil engineering or consultancy environment is desirable.
  • Strong knowledge of AutoCAD and Civil 3D.
  • Experience with drainage, utilities, roads or land development projects.
  • Knowledge of hydraulic and SuDS design, with Causeway Flow experience advantageous.
  • Good understanding of civil engineering drawings and design standards.
  • Strong attention to detail and commitment to quality.
  • Excellent interpersonal and communication skills.
  • Good organisational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities.
  • A positive, proactive and professional approach.
  • Ability to work effectively both independently and as part of a multidisciplinary team.

What’s on Offer

  • Opportunity to work on diverse civil infrastructure and development projects.
  • Exposure to experienced engineers and multidisciplinary teams.
  • Opportunities for technical development and career progression.
  • Supportive and collaborative working environment.
  • Training and professional development opportunities.
  • Competitive salary and comprehensive benefits package.
