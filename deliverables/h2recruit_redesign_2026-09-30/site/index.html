How we work

One consultant.
One search.
Start to signature.

No hand-offs between a "resourcer" and an "account manager". The person who took the brief is the person who calls you with the shortlist.

  1. 01

    Consultation, not a form

    A detailed conversation about stage, territory, comp structure and what "good" has looked like before. For candidates, the same depth on what you actually want next.

  2. 02

    Benchmark against the market

    We are placing these roles every week in London and New York, so the comp bands and expectations we give you are this quarter's, not last year's.

  3. 03

    A shortlist of three to five

    Every candidate interviewed by a former seller, prepped, and matched on stage and culture. Nobody sent "just to see".

  4. 04

    Offer, counter-offer, onboarding

    We manage the offer stage on both sides and stay in the loop after start date. Referral scheme: £250 per placement for successful candidate referrals.
