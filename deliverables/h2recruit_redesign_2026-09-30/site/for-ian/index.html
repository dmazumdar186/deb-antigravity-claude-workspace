The redesign at h2recruit-redesign.pages.dev is a concept on my own domain. Nothing touches h2recruit.co.uk. All copy, roles, salary bands, addresses and names come from your live site; the outreach and market-read numbers are illustrative placeholders until you give me real ones.

Two things that are not on your site at all

  • Outreach feed. The section you replied to my email about. Every open role feeds a personalised sequence to matched sellers; replies land warm with the consultant. The page shows a replay of one night's run. I can plug it into your real pipeline.
  • Market read. A client types the seat and gets a comp band, candidate source, shortlist time and network match in seconds, then a consultant calls. It runs on a rule engine today and can run on an LLM with your salary-report data behind it.
