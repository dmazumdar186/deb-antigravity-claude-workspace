## 0. Non-negotiables (from the panel)
1. Every fact on the public site is verbatim from BRIEF.md or data/jobs.json. No invented testimonials, logos, placement counts, salaries, bios, dates. No "nuclear". No CRO number on the site (third-party sourced; recommended in the note only).
2. Zero mention of AI / Claude / automation / "AI-powered" anywhere on the public pages. The build story lives ONLY on `for-keith/index.html`.
3. Keep Gaia's own lines: "Connecting People with Purpose" survives as the *closing* line of the hero sequence or the statement section (it is their line, don't kill it); "Schedule a confidential one to one chat" is the primary contact CTA verbatim; phone numbers front and centre (Dublin +353 1 211 8940 · Clare +353 65 672 9020). No chatbot, no calendar widget.
4. Never slag Hidden Depth. No "old site vs new" comparisons on public pages. The footer has no agency credit; it has a small line "Redesign concept by Debanjan Mazumdar · September 2026".
5. Every job card links out to the real `https://gaiatalent.com/jobs/<slug>/` page and the page says "Live roles as of 15 September 2026". No fake apply form.
6. Team section uses the five real portraits (assets/team-*.jpg in scratchpad/assets) with the exact titles. One factual line each: their verbatim title only, plus for Isadora "Consultant on all current live roles" (true per jobs.json: 63/63). No other invented lines. Bios are flagged in the note as "for Keith to supply".
7. Forms: phone-first. The form submit builds a `mailto:` with the fields prefilled and shows an honest notice ("Your email app will open with this message. Prefer to talk? Call Dublin or Clare."). No fake "message sent" toast. Recipient: info@gaiatalent.com is UNVERIFIED — use the Cloudflare-obfuscated address? We can't decode it; so the mailto uses `keith@gaiatalent.com`? Also unverified. DECISION: mailto goes to nobody invented: use a `data-mailto` attribute set to "" and the notice says "Email address to be confirmed by Gaia" ONLY on the Keith note; on the public page the form falls back to opening `tel:` links + a copy-to-clipboard of the message text. Simplest honest behaviour: on submit, show the composed message in a panel with a "Copy message" button and the two phone numbers, plus a mailto link that the build script fills from `config.json` (`contact_email`, default empty → link hidden). Ship with it empty.
8. Static, relative paths only (no leading `/`), works from file:// and under https://dmazumdar186.github.io/deb-antigravity-claude-workspace/gaia/ . `<meta name="robots" content="noindex,nofollow">` on every page (the demo must not compete with gaiatalent.com in search).
9. Reduced-motion, no-JS and <800px degrade ladders on every animated section (see §6).
10. No typos. Copy is proofread; British/Irish spelling (organisation, specialise, programme).

Voice words: instrumented · weatherproofed · load-bearing. Scene: a site engineer at 7am on a Co. Clare wind-farm access track, overcast Atlantic light. Lane: Anduril-grade technical seriousness × Ordnance Survey map-legend logic, applied to civil/environmental recruitment. Bans honoured (no Inter/DM/Space Grotesk/Fraunces etc.). Scale:

## 5. Hero canvas — "one drawing that rearranges itself"
`<canvas class="flow__scene" aria-hidden="true">` behind the copy, 2D context, DPR-aware, sized to the stage. A single horizon polyline of 32 vertices; each state defines a y-profile; y is lerped by sub-progress with ease-out-quart. Sky is a vertical gradient whose two stops are lerped per state (state colours: intro deep navy→navy-800; wind cool grey-blue; solar navy with amber horizon glow (marker at 20% alpha); water blue-teal; tidal dark teal; transport slate; ecology green-tinted navy). Ground fill = navy-950 with a 1px green-400 horizon stroke at 60% alpha. Elements (drawn with the SAME palette: strokes green-400/white at low alpha, fills navy-800; no photoreal):
- wind: 5 turbine masts at fixed x on the horizon; blades rotate with time (2 rpm), mast height scales in with state weight; hydro: a small dam line + reservoir plane behind.
- solar: rows of thin panel slabs (parallelograms) whose tilt lerps 8°→26° with weight; a faint sun disc rises.
- water: foreground water plane rises (rect from bottom, alpha .35) with 3 sine ripple lines; flood-plain contours.
- tidal/storage: 6 barrage gates across the water (rect scaleY), a battery-block outline row on shore; slow wave.
- transport: a greenway path curve drawing in (dash offset) with waypoint dots; a simple bridge span (two piers + deck) on the horizon.
- ecology: a faint survey grid over the scene (alpha .12), 6 small bird glyphs (two-stroke V) drifting, a tree line.
Each element has weight w_i = smooth window around its state (triangular with 15% overlap) → opacity/scale; nothing hard-cuts. Copy panels crossfade at the 50% point of each leg (same as demo's film.js). Step rail `--fill` per step. Time-based motion (blades, ripples, birds) keeps running at ~30fps only while the stage is in view and `!document.hidden`.
Degrade: <800px → canvas still draws but with 3 fewer elements per state and the track shortens to 4×100vh... NO — keep it simple: <800px = no pinning; each state becomes a normal stacked block with a static SVG illustration of its sector (six small inline SVGs, same style, ≤2KB each) and the same copy + role card. reduced-motion → same as mobile at all widths. no-JS → same stacked layout (CSS `html:not(.has-js)`).

## 10. Data notes (from the dataset build)
- jobs.json has 63 records (sitemap said 64; one slug was a duplicate/removed). Use 63 everywhere. Do not show salaries (only 7/63 disclose; showing some and not others misleads). - Flow role picks: match by title keywords in this order and take the first hit: wind → ["consents","renewables","principal engineer energy"]; solar → ["land liaison","project engineer renewable","project manager renewable"]; water → ["flood","hydrogeologist","water"]; tidal/storage → ["energy","renewables"] (excluding ones already used); transport → ["bridge","greenway","roads and transport","transportation"]; ecology → ["ornithology","ecologist","eia"].
