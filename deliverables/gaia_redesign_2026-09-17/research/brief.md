## The reference demo (evolution-exhibits-v4.pages.dev) — what "flow & dynamic" means to the operator
Zero libraries, ~13KB JS, ~37KB CSS, system fonts. Mechanics: fixed pill header that solidifies on scroll + page-progress bar; HERO = sticky 100svh stage inside a tall track (N×100vh) where scroll scrubs a video through N states with crossfading headline panels and a step rail (the "flow"); statement section; pinned CARD STACK (5 cards, transforms driven by scroll progress via CSS vars); 3D DRUM stepper (rotateX rolodex of 4 process steps); full-bleed testimonial band; hover-driven capabilities list; CTA; footer. Section themes alternate ink / paper / accent. Type: tight -0.06em display at clamp(46-64px, 6.5-11vw, 104-185px), line-height .8. Breakpoints: 1050 (hamburger), 799 (flatten all pinning), 480. prefers-reduced-motion and no-JS fallbacks throughout. IntersectionObserver reveals. Native <dialog> lightbox.
Operator idea: use the scroll flow to move through renewable-energy sectors (solar, wind, water/hydro, grid, etc.). NOTE: nuclear is not a Gaia sector; use sectors Gaia actually recruits for.

## Constraints
- Static HTML/CSS/JS only (GitHub Pages). Must be mobile-responsive. No paid assets. Images: Gaia's own logo SVG + B Corp logo are downloaded; their stock heroes are abstract; free imagery via Unsplash URLs must be verified; SVG/canvas/generative scenes are allowed and encouraged.
- Keith is not a techie: the site must not read as an "AI headspace thing". It must feel like a serious recruitment brand that happens to be built at the frontier.
- Deb's operating doctrine ("impeccable" design skill): no cream/beige default, no gradient text, no eyebrow-kicker on every section, no identical icon-card grids, no hero-metric template, no 01/02/03 scaffolding, ban Inter/DM Sans/Space Grotesk/Playfair/Fraunces etc, contrast ≥4.5:1, reduced-motion required, imagery required, one signature motion moment beats scattered reveals, a named aesthetic reference before choosing colour.
