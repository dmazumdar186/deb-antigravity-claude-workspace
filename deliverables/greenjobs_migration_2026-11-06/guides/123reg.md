## C. Change the nameservers (repeat for each of the 8 domains)
Due by: {DATE}
From https://123-reg.co.uk/support/domains/how-do-i-change-the-nameservers-for-my-domain-name (read 2026-11-20):
1. Make sure the domain is not locked first (the article links to a separate lock guide; those steps: UNVERIFIED — confirm on screen).
2. Log in to the 123 Reg Control Panel.
3. **My Products** page, scroll to **Domains**, find the domain, click **Manage**.
4. On **Domain Portfolio** click **DNS**, then **Nameservers**, then **Change Nameservers**.
5. Choose **I'll use my own nameservers**. Type {NS1} and {NS2}. Click **Save**.
6. Screenshot the old values BEFORE saving.

Do NOT touch: MX or email settings, renewal or auto-renew, transfer lock (turn it back on if you had to switch it off), contacts.
