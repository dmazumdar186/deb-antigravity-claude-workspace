## Checked and found fine

- **Layout and overflow:** No horizontal overflow on any page, device or edition (document width equals viewport width). No broken images, and no clipped headings or buttons were detected.
- **Console and network:** No console errors except the expected 404s on the 404 page and the broken worker job URLs. No failed requests to the API (`/api/config`, `/api/jobs`, `/api/stats`).
- **Home hero (all devices):** It matches the approved design. The photo hero is there, with wind turbines on IE and solar plus turbines on UK. The serif headline, search box, stats pill, B Corp card and job-alert strip are present. The logo wall carousel has its Pause control. The sector cards have sparklines on laptop. The UK home has the region map.
- **Jobs list:** The filter rail on laptop and the bottom-sheet Filters drawer on phones both work. The sector select narrows the list and writes it to the URL. Keyword search works on all devices: IE "ecolog" gives 12 of 88 and UK "engineer" gives 29 of 98, and both write `?q=` to the URL. The Map view renders the Ireland county map and the UK region map, with counts, on laptop and phones. Quick job match is present.
- **Other pages:** The sectors, employers (logo wall, comparison table, Post-a-job preview), career compass and dashboard pages render and look the same as the approved design.
- **Alert form validation:** Empty and malformed emails are blocked by native validation ("Please fill out this field" and "Please include an '@'"). It does not send anything (see M2).
- **Cookie banner:** It shows on first visit on every device. It offers Accept and Settings (Settings opens essential and analytics toggles), and it is dismissed after Accept. On iPhone 14 it covers the stats pill until it is dismissed, which is expected.
- **404 page:** "Nothing grows here." with IE and UK edition links. It returns status 404 on both editions.
- **Edition switch:** The IE/UK toggle is present in the header and footer.
