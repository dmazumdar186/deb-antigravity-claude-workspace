Twelve questions for you

  1. Domains. Which domains are on the Strategies contract: just greenjobs.ie and greenjobs.co.uk, or other sites too? If there are others, I'll scope those separately so this plan stays on track.
  2. DNS. Who manages DNS and domain registration for them, and who there can make changes?
  3. Email. Who hosts the email on those domains (for example info@)?
  4. Exports. Who on your side can log in to Strategies and run the 'Download Reports' exports with me (five data types per site, in batches)?
  5. Retention. How far back should we keep candidate and application records? Strategies flagged data protection; I'd suggest two years.
  6. Posting today. Who posts jobs today: your team, advertisers themselves, or both?
  7. Posting tools. Which of Broadbean, Idibu and LogicMelon are used, who owns those accounts, and which CRM do you post from?
  8. Applications. Where should applications go: one inbox, the consultant on each job, or the advertiser?
  9. Volumes. Roughly how many jobs a month, live candidate accounts, alert subscribers and employer accounts?
  10. Payments. Do any advertisers pay online today (featured jobs, packages)?
  11. Analytics and logos. Who can give me access to Google Analytics and Search Console for both boards, and can I use the employer logos and testimonials that are on the current boards?
  12. Contract. Who signs the contract and data processing agreement on your side? If both are back by Wednesday 11 November, the exports start in time. If it runs later, the 3 December date still stays as long as DNS access is in hand, and we agree in writing which items move to the second phase.
