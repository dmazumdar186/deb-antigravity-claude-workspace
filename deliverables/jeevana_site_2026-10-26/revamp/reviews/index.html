      How reviews work

      Reviews are written by visitors to this site and are not verified. They are shown with their date, newest first; the three latest reviews rated 4 or 5 stars are featured at the top. A review that contains a link, contact details, offensive or advertising words, text in capitals or long runs of the same character, that is signed with the name Jeevana, or that arrives during an unusual burst of reviews, is checked by Jeevana before it appears. Jeevana may hide or remove a review.
