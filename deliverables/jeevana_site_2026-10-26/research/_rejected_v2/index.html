The practice

Every class holds three things: posture, movement and breath.

Yoga in Paris, taught from the tradition.

Jeevana teaches yoga in Paris. Classes in Paris — group and private. Each class follows the traditional order of posture, breath and rest, and adapts to the body in the room.

Traditional roots

The Yoga Sūtras, the breath and the sequence. Sūrya Namaskāra is taught as the tradition teaches it: breath and movement together.

For every body

Beginners are welcome. Every posture has an adaptation, and the breath sets the pace, not the clock.
