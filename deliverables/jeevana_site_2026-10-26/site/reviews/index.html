      How reviews work

      Reviews are written by visitors to this site and are not verified. They are shown with their date, newest first; the latest reviews rated 4 or 5 stars are featured at the top. A review that contains a link, contact details or offensive words is checked by Jeevana before it appears. Jeevana may remove reviews that are abusive, off-topic or not about her classes.
