१About me

I’m Jeevana.
I teach Hatha Yoga in Paris.

I teach Hatha Yoga to teenagers and adults of every level, in French or English. My classes are accessible: no perfect postures, just a practice that lets you progress at your own pace and feel better in your body. I take time to explain, and I shape each session around you.

My training

Certified yoga instructor (Moniteur de Yoga), Tapovan↗

I trained in Hatha Yoga through an intensive 500-hour programme at Tapovan Open University Yoga & Ayurveda, in Normandy.

What my training covered

  • International Yoga protocol and traditional āsana
  • Prāṇāyāma and the yoga of the breath
  • Yoga Nidra, deep yogic relaxation
  • Dhāraṇā and dhyāna: concentration and meditation
  • Ayur Yoga and the basics of Ayurveda
  • Jñāna, bhakti and karma yoga
  • Integral yoga
  • Pedagogy of yoga and teaching placements

Tapovan, in the pays de Caux, has lived Yoga and Ayurveda for more than 40 years. Its guiding principle: nothing can be taught; the teacher is a help and a guide.

I have taught at Tapovan Paris 15e and at Tapovan Normandie, then at home in Paris 19e. Today I give individual classes.

२The practice

Less striving.
More listening.

योगश्चित्तवृत्तिनिरोधः

yogaś citta-vṛtti-nirodhaḥ

Yoga is the stilling of the movements of the mind.

Yoga Sūtra 1.2

A session with me follows a simple shape. We begin by centring and breathing, move through postures adapted to your level, and end in relaxation or meditation. A posture is not an image to reproduce; it is a place to explore steadiness and ease. We go at your pace, and every breath is a chance to listen and begin again.

Aims
Starting yoga, mobility and flexibility, unwinding after work, easing stress, supporting a sport such as running.
Levels
All levels, teenagers and adults.
Languages
French or English.
Where
At mine in Paris 19e; at yours, at home or a public place within 10 km; or online by webcam.
Format
Individual, or a small group on request.
