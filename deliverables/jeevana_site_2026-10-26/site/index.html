01 —

The practice

Every class holds three things: posture, movement and breath.

  • The breath sets the pace

    Each movement rides an inhale or an exhale. The breath sets the pace, not the clock.

  • A sequence

    Each class follows the traditional order of posture, breath and rest, and adapts to the body in the room.

  • Stillness

    Breathing practice and a closing rest, where the class settles.

  • Beginners welcome

    Beginners are welcome. Every posture has an adaptation.

02 —

Classes & registration

  • Group class

    A small group, all levels, one sequence practised together.

  • Private session

    One to one, at your pace: a first practice or a specific need.

  • Workshop

    A longer session on one theme: the breath, a family of postures, the twelve positions.
