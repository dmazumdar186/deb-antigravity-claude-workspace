01 / The practice

Less striving.
More listening.

योगश्चित्तवृत्तिनिरोधः

yogaś citta-vṛtti-nirodhaḥ

Yoga is the stilling of the movements of the mind.

Yoga Sūtra 1.2

Jeevana teaches yoga in Paris. The practice begins with attention: noticing the ground, the rhythm of breathing, and the space between movements. A posture is not an image to reproduce, but a place to explore steadiness and ease. Movement and stillness belong together; effort can sit alongside softness. From a simple seated pause to a flowing sequence, the invitation is to stay curious about what is present. There is no need to rush towards a shape. Each breath offers another moment to listen, adjust, and begin again with fresh attention.
