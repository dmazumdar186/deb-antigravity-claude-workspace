02 — About

Yoga in Paris, taught from the tradition.

Jeevana teaches yoga in Paris: group classes, private sessions and workshops, in a studio and outdoors. Classes follow the traditional order of posture, breath and rest, and adapt to the body in the room.

Traditional roots

The Yoga Sūtras, the breath and the sequence. Sūrya Namaskāra is taught as the tradition teaches it: breath and movement together.

For every body

Beginners are welcome. Every posture has an adaptation, and the breath sets the pace, not the clock.

In Paris

In a studio and outdoors, under the Paris sky. The studio address will be published here.
