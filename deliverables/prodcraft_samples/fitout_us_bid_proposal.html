01 · Scope of work

What we will build

The work covers a full interior build-out of floors 11 and 12 per the architect's construction documents. Base building core, shell and restrooms are by the landlord and outside this bid.

Floor 11 · about 7,200 sq ft

  • Reception and waiting area with feature millwork desk and a glass wall to the café.
  • Café and pantry: millwork, solid-surface counters, sink, dishwasher and ice-maker connections.
  • Two conference rooms (12 and 8 seats) with acoustic insulation to deck and AV backing.
  • Open office for 44 workstations; furniture by owner, power and data poke-throughs by us.

Floor 12 · about 6,800 sq ft

  • Eight private offices with glass fronts and sliding doors.
  • Boardroom (16 seats) and one 6-seat conference room.
  • Open office for 38 workstations, two phone rooms, IDF room with dedicated cooling.

Building systems

  • HVAC: extend base-building VAV distribution, 22 new VAV boxes with controls tied to the BAS, supplemental split system for the IDF.
  • Electrical: new panelboards from the base-building riser, LED lighting with occupancy and daylight controls, floor boxes in conference rooms.
  • Plumbing: café and pantry fixtures off the existing wet column.
  • Fire protection: relocate and add heads to suit the new layout; fire alarm devices added per code.

Throughout

  • Full-time superintendent on site, daily cleanup, weekly owner meeting and three-week look-ahead.
  • Permit expediting with the City of Chicago Department of Buildings.
  • Close-out: as-builts, O&M manuals, warranties and a punch list closed within 10 working days.

05 · Assumptions & clarifications

What this price is based on

  1. Work is based on the architect's CDs dated 14 October 2026 and Addenda 1–2. Changes after that date are priced as change orders.
  2. Normal working hours are 7:00 am to 3:30 pm, Monday to Friday. Core drilling and noisy work after hours is included at 40 hours, as required by the landlord's rules.
  3. The landlord provides one freight elevator for our exclusive use 6:00–7:00 am and 3:30–6:00 pm, and shared use in between.
  4. The base-building HVAC, electrical riser and fire alarm panel have capacity for the design as drawn. Upgrades found needed are not included.
  5. Long-lead items (glass fronts, light fixtures, VAV boxes) are released on notice to proceed. Pricing assumes release by 7 December 2026.
  6. Floor flatness is assumed within the tolerance for the specified flooring. Floor prep is included up to 3,000 sq ft of skim coat.
  7. Allowances: millwork hardware $6,500 and signage $4,000, included in the trade lines above and reconciled at close-out.
  8. Owner-furnished items are delivered and stored by the owner; we coordinate delivery windows.
  9. Unused general conditions and unused allowances are returned to the owner under the GMP.

Open item for owner decision. Café appliance package: the CDs show owner-furnished appliances. If you would like us to supply them, we will price it as an alternate within 3 working days.

07 · Terms & validity

Commercial terms

  • Validity. This bid is valid for 45 days from 3 November 2026. Subcontractor pricing on glazing and electrical is held to that date only.
  • Contract. AIA A102 with A201 general conditions, as modified by mutual agreement. GMP $2,358,314 for the base bid plus accepted alternates.
  • Payment. Monthly progress billing on AIA G702/G703, net 30. Retainage 10% to 50% complete, then 5%, released at substantial completion less punch-list value.
  • Changes. Priced at cost plus the 4.5% fee and 1.2% insurance, with backup, before work proceeds.
  • Warranty. One year on workmanship from substantial completion; manufacturer warranties passed through.
  • Insurance. Certificates naming owner and landlord as additional insured, delivered before mobilization.
