02 · Concept

“Closer Together”

A theme that works twice: closer to the customer, and closer as one team after a year of growth through hiring. Everything on stage points the same way, toward the people in the room.

Look

Warm neutrals with one strong blue. Big type, short lines. Every graphic is built to read from the back row.

Story arc

Day 1: where we are and where we go. Day 2: how we win, deal by deal. Day 3: who made it happen. Each day opens with a 90-second film.

Room energy

Crescent seating and a thrust bring the stage 20 ft closer to the audience. Walk-in music and lighting change by day.

Signature moments

The number reveal. The 2027 target builds across the full LED wall, region by region, as each sales leader walks on.

Customer on stage. A live 12-minute conversation with a customer, shot on three cameras for IMAG.

Awards walk. Winners enter through the audience on a lit runway to the thrust.

03 · General session look

One wide, warm stage

A 56 ft stage with a full-width LED wall as the set itself. No scenic flats to haul; the wall changes the look for every segment.

  • Main LED wall 48 × 13.5 ft, 2.6 mm pitch, carries keynote graphics, films and live camera.
  • Two IMAG screens flank the wall so the back rows see faces, not dots.
  • Thrust stage 12 × 16 ft for fireside chats and the awards walk.
  • Lectern + confidence monitors downstage, plus a countdown clock for every presenter.
  • FOH centred two-thirds back, with sightline to the whole stage.

Room assumptions. Ballroom about 30,000 sq ft, 28 ft to the rigging grid, theatre seating for 600 with room for camera risers. Confirmed at the site visit.

08 · Team & how we work

One producer, one plan, no surprises

Executive producer

Owns the budget and is your single point of contact from sign-off to wrap.

Show producer

Runs content, speakers and rehearsals; writes the show script.

Technical director

Owns the AV design, the rigging plot and the venue relationship.

Stage manager + show caller

Keep every segment on time and every presenter ready.

Content team

Films, show graphics and presenter templates in your brand.

How we work

  • Weekly 30-minute call from sign-off, and a shared tracker with every open decision and its owner.
  • Budget changes shown before they are spent, never after.
  • Every speaker gets one rehearsal slot on the real stage, with a confidence monitor and clock.
  • Wrap report within ten working days of show close, with final costs against budget.
