01 · Scope and approach

Our understanding of the works

The building is delivered to Cat A: raised floor, suspended ceiling, LED lighting, four-pipe fan coils and a fire alarm system. Our works adapt it to the designer's Cat B layout across both floors.

Level 4 · client and meeting floor

  • Reception with feature desk, waiting lounge and glazed client suite of five meeting rooms.
  • Main tea point and breakout with banquette seating and joinery.
  • Two quiet rooms, wellbeing room and a comms room with dedicated cooling.

Level 5 · workplace floor

  • Open-plan desks for 96, with lockers and two focus booths.
  • Six partner offices with glazed fronts, two team rooms and a satellite tea point.

Services adaptations

  • Re-position and add fan coil units to suit cellular rooms; extract to tea points.
  • Re-circuit lighting with presence detection; feature lighting under PS2.
  • Floor boxes to desks and meeting rooms; containment for the client's IT supplier.
  • Fire alarm detection and sounders adapted to the new room layout.

How we will run it

  • Full-time site manager and a contracts manager visiting twice weekly.
  • Logistics agreed with building management: goods lift booking, loading bay slots, noisy works before 08:00 and after 18:00 under PS3.
  • Considerate Constructors registration; weekly progress report to the project manager.

05 · Qualifications and exclusions

Qualifications

  1. Our tender is based on the documents listed on the cover. Design development after the tender date is valued as a variation.
  2. The Cat A installation is complete, commissioned and in working order on start on site. Defects in the landlord's works are dealt with under PS4.
  3. Fan coil units are re-used where shown; the number of new units is limited to the 14 shown on the mechanical layouts.
  4. Normal working hours are 08:00–18:00 Monday to Friday. Weekend and out-of-hours work is covered by PS3 only.
  5. The goods lift and loading bay are available for our use for at least 4 hours each working day.
  6. Loose furniture, AV and IT are by the client, installed after practical completion as shown on the programme.
  7. No allowance for asbestos: the building is a recent Cat A shell with a clear refurbishment survey.

Exclusions

  • VAT
  • Loose furniture, AV equipment and IT cabling
  • Landlord's legal costs and service charge during the works
  • Planning or building regulations fees beyond PS1
  • Works to landlord areas, risers, toilets and the roof
  • Specialist acoustic testing beyond one test per room type
  • Liquidated damages above 5% of the contract sum in total

06 · Tender validity and terms

Commercial terms

  • Tender validity. 60 days from the return date of 3 November 2026.
  • Contract. JCT Minor Works Building Contract 2016 with the amendments in the tender pack, save for the qualifications in section 05.
  • Payment. Monthly valuations; payment 14 days from the due date. Retention 3%, half released at practical completion and half at the end of the 12-month rectification period.
  • Liquidated damages. Accepted at £2,500 per week, capped at 5% of the contract sum.
  • Insurance. Contractor's all risks to full reinstatement value of the works; employer's liability £10m; public liability £10m.
  • Accreditations. Considerate Constructors registered; site manager SMSTS-trained; all operatives hold CSCS cards.
