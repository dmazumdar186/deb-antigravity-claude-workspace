01 · Program overview

A reward that feels personal, not packaged

Lisbon in May is warm, bright and walkable, with direct flights from most European hubs and from several US gateways. The program balances group moments with free time, so qualifiers feel rewarded, not managed.

The shape of the trip

  • Day 1 · arrive, settle in, welcome dinner with a view over the river.
  • Day 2 · Lisbon by tram and on foot, long lunch, free afternoon.
  • Day 3 · Sintra palaces and the Atlantic coast, free evening.
  • Day 4 · free morning, awards and gala dinner at an exclusive-use venue.
  • Day 5 · late breakfast and departures.

Why it works for a sales group

  • Two group meals that feel like events, two that feel like a night out with friends.
  • Free time every day, with a concierge desk to book anything extra.
  • The awards moment sits inside the gala, not in a hotel ballroom.
  • Every activity is low-effort physically and works for partners too.

Program at a glance

4 group dinners or lunches · 2 guided experiences · 1 gala with entertainment · 8 on-site staff plus a program director · 24/7 hotel hospitality desk · all ground transport included.

03 · Hotel options

Three ways to stay

All three hold 120 single-occupancy rooms for 12–16 May 2027 at the rates shown, including breakfast and city tax. Named properties are confirmed with the contract; descriptions below are examples of the category.

Option A

Riverside five-star

Five-star on the river, e.g. a waterfront property in the Cais do Sodré area.

  • All rooms with river or city view
  • Private room for the welcome desk and briefings
  • Spa and rooftop pool
  • 10 minutes on foot to the old town

€520 per room night

Option B

Design hotel, Chiado

Boutique design hotel in Chiado, e.g. a converted 19th-century building.

  • Characterful rooms, smaller footprint
  • Whole-hotel buyout possible for 120
  • Walking distance to everything in the program
  • Rooftop bar for a private hospitality evening

€410 per room night

Option C

Coastal resort, Cascais

Coastal resort in Cascais, e.g. an ocean-front five-star 30 minutes from Lisbon.

  • Sea-view rooms and a large pool deck
  • Beach and promenade on the doorstep
  • Adds 30–40 minutes of coach time on days 2 and 4
  • Best for a slower, resort-style feel

€360 per room night

Our recommendation: Option A. The river location keeps every transfer short and gives the group a shared view to come back to each evening.

06 · Terms

Booking terms

  • Validity. This proposal and the hotel rates are held for 21 days from 3 November 2026, subject to availability on signing.
  • Payment. 30% on signing; 40% 90 days before arrival; 30% 30 days before arrival.
  • Rooming list. Final names 30 days before arrival. Up to 10% of rooms can be released without charge until 60 days before arrival.
  • Cancellation. More than 120 days: deposit retained. 120–61 days: 50% of the program total. 60 days or less: 100%.
  • Changes. Changes after signing are confirmed in writing with any cost before they are made.
