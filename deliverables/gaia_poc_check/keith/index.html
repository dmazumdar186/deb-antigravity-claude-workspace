  • chartership evidence (Engineers Ireland) — Chartered with Engineers Ireland (CEng with MIEI or FIEI), stated in a public source.
  • residence evidence (Republic of Ireland) — Lives in the Republic of Ireland, stated directly (a project in Ireland or the firm's office does not count).
  • discipline match — structural, structures, structural engineer, civil and structural, civil & structural, bridge, reinforced concrete, structural design as the person's own discipline.
  • seniority evidence — At least 8 years' experience stated publicly.
  • Senior Engineer ceiling — No higher than Senior Engineer grade, and no more than 15 years.
  • employer sector match — Works at an engineering consultancy, not a client-side body.
