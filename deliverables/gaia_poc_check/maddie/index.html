What runs before the screener, and how it stays out of its way

For Maddie, who built Gaia's inbound screener. This is the technical companion to Keith's one-page check. It describes a verification stage that sits upstream of the inbound flow, writes into Recruit CRM in the shape Maddie already expects, and never touches an applicant.

Where it sits

Gaia · existsNamesRecruit CRM AI Sourcing, LinkedIn Recruiter, a consultant's own list. Exported as CSV or pasted.
Prodcraft · thisCheckLocate public evidence per name, extract claims with a verbatim quote, validate the quote against the cached page, apply the brief as hard gates.
Prodcraft · thisHand-offPASS names upserted in Recruit CRM at stage "Sourced" with an evidence note and the Article 14 date. Dry-run by default.
Gaia · Maddie's screenerScreeningA reply of "interested" moves the candidate to the stage the screener already listens on. Its trigger, its scoring, its call. Unchanged.
The boundary, stated once. Maddie's screener owns everything from "a person is in the CRM and talking to Gaia" onward. This stage owns "is this name worth a consultant's hour, and what is the proof". It produces a candidate record and a note; it does not score CVs, does not invite to screening, does not send. If a sourced candidate entering at "Sourced" would fire the inbound trigger twice, the stage name is a config value and we pick one that does not.

The evidence rule

No quote, no claim. Every claim about a person carries a verbatim evidence_quote (12 to 400 characters) and a source_doc_id. The validator re-reads the cached document and rejects any quote that is not present character for character. Rejected claims are dropped, never softened; the drop rate is reported on the scorecard. Gates only see validated claims.

Claim(claim_id, subject_person_id, dimension, assertion ≤ 280 chars,
      evidence_quote 12–400 chars, source_doc_id, source_url,
      confidence: direct | inferred)
ValidatedClaim(… , quote_verified: bool)
GateResult(gate_id, passed, basis: claim_id, note)

Only confidence = direct claims can satisfy a hard gate. Inferred claims are shown on the card as "inferred, not directly stated" and never pass anything.

A composition guard refuses any delivery whose grades or locations violate the brief. That guard is the one-line check that would have caught the 20 August list. The brief is a typed object (JobSpec with SeniorityBand and LocationRule); CLI overrides --max-grade, --counties, --strict-location mutate it before any stage runs, so a re-cut is a gate re-run on cached evidence, no model call.

Intake for names Gaia already has

New in this proof of concept. A CSV or pasted list (name, employer, title, LinkedIn URL; only name is required) is normalised (case-folded, accent-stripped, post-nominals removed, O'Reilly and O’Reilly identical) and matched against the assessed pool. Matched names are gated immediately and offline. Unmatched names go to discovery: a search scoped to name plus employer, a fetch of the person's own page and the firm's people page, extraction, validation, gates. Discovery is built on the same pattern the pipeline uses for technical evidence and is budget-capped per run; the proof of concept ran on names already in the pool, at zero model spend.

A 50-name list therefore costs on the order of EUR 5 to 15 and returns inside an hour. Every provider call is metered against a per-run and a cumulative ceiling and the run stops, loudly, when a ceiling is hit.

Recruit CRM adapter

  • Endpoints used: create or update candidate, add note, assign to job. Update writes only empty fields; a consultant's data is never overwritten. Dedupe on email or LinkedIn URL.
  • Dry-run is the default: every write is appended to an audit log (logs/recruit_crm_audit.jsonl) and nothing goes over the wire. Live mode needs RECRUIT_CRM_API_KEY (Business plan or above) and an explicit flag. Write cap of 50 per run in both modes.
  • Field names are marked assumed until the first live run, which is treated as a contract test against your account, with you watching.
  • On the Pro plan the same payload goes through Zapier's Recruit CRM app or the CSV import. Same columns either way.

Replies

Rules first, model fallback, opt-out honoured: interested, not now, no, question, out of office, bounce. "Not now" snoozes 90 days and writes a note; STOP closes the record and erases. This is the one place a model is allowed near a customer message, and its output is a label, never a sent message. Any send is a consultant, from their own seat.

Verification of the work itself

  • Unit and contract tests across gates, validator, identity, intake, adapters and rendering; the suite runs on every change.
  • An acceptance gate reads the finished deliverable as a managing director would: every claim has a quote and a source link, no client-side firm in the shortlist, links resolve, gaps admitted.
  • Independent adversarial audits count from source; every substantive finding so far became a regression test.
  • Deterministic scorecard per delivery: composition violations (must be 0), quote drop rate, grade precision, residence precision, cost per delivered card.
Honest limits. The Engineers Ireland register is not automated until its terms are checked; chartership is currently evidenced from the person's own post-nominals and the employer's page, and the card says which. Coverage for the 8 to 15 year grade needs a licensed people-data source; the public record is senior by construction. Both are pilot-scope decisions, not promises.
