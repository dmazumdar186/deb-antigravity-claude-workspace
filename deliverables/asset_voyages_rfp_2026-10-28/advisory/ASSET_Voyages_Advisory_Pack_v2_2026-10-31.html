Why a modular monolith, and why not Directus or microservices. Five users, ≈ 300 listings, low traffic: separate services add network failure modes, deploy coordination and monitoring for no scaling benefit. One repo, three Worker entry points, shared typed modules, adapters at the edges. Directus + same Astro front end: mature editor, roles, revisions, media; maybe −25 to −30 h on the Console; but a server + Postgres (≈ €10–30/month, estimate), its patching and uptime become mine, ≈ 16 h setup, the vault becomes a permission setting rather than a structural split, and a second runtime to hand over. WordPress + ACF (keep current): cheapest for editors, but plugin surface, PHP hosting, GM&E isolation harder to prove; L0 must assess it honestly. Directus is the call I most want challenged.
