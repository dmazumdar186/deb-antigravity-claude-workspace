# Questions for ASSET Voyages (25, MECE)

Seven groups, no overlap, covering the whole project: the decision, what exists today, content, enquiries, the Congress catalogue, partners and options, running the site. Cross-checked against the brief's "Decisions to confirm before contract" table (budget and schedule → 1–2; existing technology → 4–5; content and languages → 8–11; commercial enquiries → 12; partners → 16, 19; operations → 13, 22, 25).

**1. Timing, budget and decisions**
1. When is the proposal due, and is there a date the new site has to be live by?
2. Is there a budget range for the core site and the Congress catalogue? A rough bracket is enough.
3. Who at ASSET approves each stage (design, launch), and how many working days should we plan for each approval?

**2. Today's website and accounts**
4. Who manages the current site today (CMS, hosting, domain), and are those accounts in ASSET's name?
5. Should the new site be on asset-voyagesdaffaires.com, assetvoyages.com, or both?
6. Could you give us read-only access to your website statistics (Google Analytics, Search Console)? Which trackers (LinkedIn, Meta…) do you want to keep?
7. Does Selectour ask for anything on your website (logos, legal mentions, brand rules)?

**3. Content and your offer**
8. Will your team write the new French copy, or do you want us to rewrite the existing pages? Roughly how many pages do you expect?
9. Do you have approved photos, client logos, testimonials and figures we can publish?
10. For small businesses, is there a defined simplified offer to present? And which assistance services exist today (for example an emergency line, and its hours)?
11. Which journeys should be in English first, and who will translate?

**4. Enquiries and client data**
12. Who should get each type of enquiry (Business Travel, MICE, Congresses)? Do you use a CRM today?
13. How long may we keep enquiry data, and who handles personal-data requests at ASSET?

**5. Congress catalogue**
14. Do you already have a list of events (the 300 mentioned in the brief)? Could you share 10 sample rows and tell us where the listings come from?
15. Who at ASSET approves a congress for sale? Is it one person or several?
16. Once a congress is approved for sale, what can we show publicly about Air France–KLM fares? A "preferential fares" badge, or only "request a quote"?
17. When a congress is over, should its page stay online as an archive, or come down?
18. Should one quote request cover several travellers leaving from different cities (for example, a lab sending doctors from Lyon and Paris)?

**6. Partners and options**
19. Your current client area says "Connexion Traveldoo", while the brief mentions Wondermiles. Is Wondermiles replacing Traveldoo? For launch, is a "Client login" link enough, and do you have a technical contact there if we look at a single login later?
20. For the AI option, what should it do first? Find new congresses for your team to review, or something else?

**7. Security and running the site**
21. Does your team sign in with Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace? We'd use it for a secure two-step login to the admin area.
22. Must the site and its data be hosted in the EU or in France? Do your clients send you security questionnaires we should plan for?
23. Who besides you will edit the site day to day? We need to know so we can plan the training.
24. What kind of changes do you expect each month (new pages, offers, congress updates)?
25. What support hours do you need (office hours only, or evenings and weekends during congress season)? And how long can the site be unavailable before it hurts your business?
