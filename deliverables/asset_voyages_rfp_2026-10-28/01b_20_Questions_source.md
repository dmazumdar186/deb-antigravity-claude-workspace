# Questions for ASSET Voyages (30, MECE)

Seven groups, no overlap, covering the whole project: the decision, today's website, content, enquiries, the Congress catalogue, partners and options, running the site. Every question carries the default we will apply if it is not answered in writing. Proof of coverage: `07_Question_Coverage_Matrix.md`.

Anything you don't answer in writing will follow the assumption shown under each question. You can change any assumption by email at any time before the proposal is signed; after that, changes follow the change process.

**1. Timing, budget and decisions**
1. When is the proposal due, and when do you plan to choose a supplier? Is there a date the new site has to be live by, or a busy period when it should not launch?  
   *If we don't hear back, we'll assume:* we send the proposal by Friday 13 November 2026, start on Monday 16 November, and launch in February 2027 (about 12 weeks). No launch between 21 December and 1 January.
2. Is there a budget range or a cap for the core site and the Congress catalogue? A rough bracket is enough.  
   *If we don't hear back, we'll assume:* we quote the core site and the Congress catalogue as your brief describes them, with every option priced separately, so you can pick.
3. Who at ASSET approves each stage (design, content, launch), and does each activity (Business Travel, MICE, Congresses) sign off its own pages? How many working days should we plan for each approval?  
   *If we don't hear back, we'll assume:* ASSET management approves each stage in writing within 3 working days, and Veena sends one combined reply for all three activities. Later approvals move the dates by the same number of days.

**2. Today's website and accounts**
4. Who looks after the current site today (an agency, a freelancer, your team), and is there a contract or notice period with them?  
   *If we don't hear back, we'll assume:* nothing in a current contract limits the project, and ASSET arranges any handover from today's provider.
5. Are the domain, hosting, email, Google Analytics, Search Console and Google Business Profile accounts in ASSET's name? Who at ASSET should own the accounts of the new site?  
   *If we don't hear back, we'll assume:* ASSET gives us access at kickoff. Every new account is opened in ASSET's name, owned by management, with Veena as day-to-day administrator. Getting back accounts held by others is up to ASSET.
6. Should the new site be on asset-voyagesdaffaires.com, assetvoyages.com, or both? Are there other web addresses or campaign pages we should know about?  
   *If we don't hear back, we'll assume:* the new site lives on asset-voyagesdaffaires.com, the address Google knows today, and assetvoyages.com points to it. No other addresses are included.
7. Could you give us read-only access to Google Analytics and Search Console? Which trackers (LinkedIn, Meta) and ad campaigns (Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads) must keep working?

8. Does Selectour ask for anything on your website (logos, legal mentions, brand rules)?  
   *If we don't hear back, we'll assume:* we show the Selectour Affaires logo and membership line as they appear on today's site, and nothing more.
