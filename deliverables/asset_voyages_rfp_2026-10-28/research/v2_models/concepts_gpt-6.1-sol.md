## 1. Le Dossier qui voyage

**Master metaphor:** A paper brief folds into a travelling dossier, carrying every decision from the first request to the final expense record. At each stop, it opens only the information needed: policy, itinerary, assistance or reporting.

### Seven-stage film

**0s — La demande**  
**“Tout commence par votre besoin.”**  
Overhead: one cream sheet, three ink checkboxes: *Affaires / Événement / Congrès*. During 0–5s, the selected box enlarges; its outline becomes a folder containing three labelled tabs.

**5s — Le cadrage**  
**“Vos règles prennent leur place.”**  
The *Affaires* tab opens: destination, dates and travel-policy fields. During 5–10s, paper strips align inside these fields; the camera tracks right as the folder’s flap becomes a comparison grid.

**10s — Les options**  
**“Comparer avant de décider.”**  
Three itinerary slips sit beside a *Tarifs négociés* stamp. No invented prices. During 10–15s, one slip slides forward; rejected alternatives tuck behind it, forming an accordion.

**15s — L’organisation**  
**“Chaque déplacement trouve son format.”**  
The accordion opens three labelled compartments: business itinerary; MICE brief marked *10 personnes et plus*; congress catalogue with *Demander un devis vol*. During 15–20s, all three fold into the same dossier silhouette.

**20s — Le départ**  
**“L’essentiel part avec vous.”**  
The closed dossier travels across a sparse map; itinerary tabs remain visible. During 20–25s, the camera follows it until an assistance tab catches on the screen edge and opens.

**25s — L’appui**  
**“Un interlocuteur, même quand tout change.”**  
An advisor contact card and *Assistance 24/7* panel unfold beside the route. During 25–30s, a bent route straightens; supporting papers slide into a final accounting pocket.

**30s — Le bilan**  
**“Le voyage finit. La visibilité reste.”**  
Final hold: the dossier lies open, displaying *Notes de frais / Factures dématérialisées / Statistiques*. Its cover becomes the next section’s headline surface.

**Pinned signature object:** The tabbed dossier. It carries actual content categories: request type, dates, policy, options, advisor contact, assistance and administrative outputs—not decorative passport stamps.

**Approach drum:** *Écouter → Cadrer → Organiser → Accompagner → Analyser*

**Why a travel manager remembers it:** It makes the invisible administrative work of travel visibly organised, contained and retrievable.

## 2. Le Fil sans rupture

**Master metaphor:** A single ink line connects a business need to its destination, winding onto a paper reel whenever complexity accumulates. ASSET’s role is to keep that thread continuous through decisions, changes and return.

### Seven-stage film

**0s — Le besoin**  
**“Un déplacement commence par un lien.”**  
A black point sits beside *Votre entreprise*. During 0–5s, it draws a line towards an empty destination label; the camera follows the advancing tip.

**5s — Le cadre**  
**“Votre politique donne la direction.”**  
The line passes through three paper gates: *Dates / Budget / Politique voyage*. During 5–10s, the gates slide into alignment, turning a tangled stroke into parallel lanes.

**10s — Les choix**  
**“Les options deviennent lisibles.”**  
Three route-lines fan out, each carrying an itinerary card. During 10–15s, one remains in accent colour; the others retract into a flat paper reel.

**15s — Les projets**  
**“À chaque projet, sa trajectoire.”**  
The reel unwinds into three lanes: *Affaires*, *MICE · dès 10 personnes*, *Congrès · devis vol*. During 15–20s, their labels ride along the lines; the camera pulls back to reveal one connected composition.

**20s — Le mouvement**  
**“Vous avancez, nous gardons le lien.”**  
The business lane crosses a simplified map; a contact tab stays tethered to it. During 20–25s, a route segment lifts into a loop, stopping beside an *Assistance 24/7* marker.

**25s — L’imprévu**  
**“Le trajet change. Le lien reste.”**  
A broken bridge symbol interrupts the illustrated route. During 25–30s, a second ink arc connects the two ends; the line winds neatly back onto its reel.

**30s — La lecture**  
**“Chaque trajet laisse une trace utile.”**  
Final hold: the reel’s paper wrapper opens into rows labelled *Dépenses / Factures / Statistiques*, connected to the completed route.

**Pinned signature object:** The paper reel and its continuous line. Attached tickets carry itinerary choices, policy constraints, service contacts and reporting categories; motion expresses continuity, not live tracking.

**Approach drum:** *Relier → Clarifier → Coordonner → Assister → Restituer*

**Why a travel manager remembers it:** The unbroken line turns “dedicated advisor plus ongoing assistance” into one immediately recognisable visual promise.

## 3. La Date prend place

**Master metaphor:** One calendar square unfolds into the complete organisation surrounding a date. What looks like a small appointment becomes a structured paper landscape, then folds back into a manageable agenda.

### Seven-stage film

**0s — La date**  
**“Une date. Tout à organiser.”**  
A large calendar grid fills the screen; one square is outlined in accent colour. During 0–5s, the camera enters that square as its four edges unfold.

**5s — L’intention**  
**“D’abord, savoir pourquoi partir.”**  
Three paper flaps appear: *Mission / Séminaire / Congrès*. During 5–10s, each reveals its request: business trip, group brief, or event search and flight quotation.

**10s — Le cadre**  
**“Donner des contours au projet.”**  
The square becomes a ruled planning sheet: dates, destination, participants, budget. During 10–15s, the rules extend into accordion folds; *Politique voyage* and *Cahier des charges* occupy separate panels.

**15s — Les pièces**  
**“Tout trouver, sans tout porter.”**  
Flat itinerary cards, a group specification sheet and a congress quotation request fill their respective panels. During 15–20s, the camera rises; the papers arrange themselves around the original date.

**20s — La coordination**  
**“Chaque élément à sa place.”**  
Thin ink connectors join each panel to an *Interlocuteur dédié* card. During 20–25s, the connectors shorten, drawing the scattered papers into a compact cross-shaped organiser.

**25s — L’accompagnement**  
**“Avant, pendant, toujours un appui.”**  
The business panel reveals *Assistance 24/7*; the MICE panel reveals *Chef de projet*. During 25–30s, the outer panels fold inward, leaving labelled tabs accessible.

**30s — La maîtrise**  
**“Votre agenda retrouve de l’espace.”**  
Final hold: everything fits inside the original calendar square. Three tabs remain visible: *Affaires / MICE / Congrès*; scrolling opens the corresponding content.

**Pinned signature object:** The expandable calendar square. It holds the real planning fields and service-specific documents; it is a navigation metaphor, not a claim that ASSET offers one integrated planning application.

**Approach drum:** *Analyser → Définir → Coordonner → Accompagner → Réussir*

**Why a travel manager remembers it:** It visualises a familiar pressure—a single date generating dozens of tasks—and shows those tasks becoming manageable.

## What I would ship

I would ship **Le Dossier qui voyage**: its transformations are concrete, legible and directly connected to ASSET’s services. It accommodates business travel, MICE and congress enquiries without pretending they share an identical booking process. The dossier can also become the homepage’s navigation system, with pinned tabs opening service details, approach cards and contact options.
