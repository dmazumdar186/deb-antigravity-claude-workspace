# ASSET Voyages — 3 Signature Concepts

---

## CONCEPT 1 — «Le Passeport du Voyage d'Affaires»

**Master metaphor:** A travel file is a passport that gets stamped at each stage of its journey. Scroll scrubs a 30-second film where a paper dossier is opened, filled, stamped, boarded, flown, landed and archived — the client watches a business trip being *issued*, like a visa.

**7-stage film storyboard** (2D vector: cream paper, ink lines, one accent red — like a French customs stamp):

- **t=0 — «Le Brief» / «Un déplacement commence par une demande»** On screen: a blank passport-sized booklet lies centrefold open on a desk surface; a hand-drawn pen (no hand) writes "PARIS → BERLIN" in the first page; the booklet's pages fan slightly. *Leg 0→5:* a stamp slides in from the corner and THUMPS the page red — camera zooms 5% per second toward the stamp.
- **t=5 — «La Politique» / «Vos règles, intégrées dès la réservation»** On screen: the booklet page becomes a boarding-pass template; policy clauses fly in as small typed lines and *slot into* the ticket like the booking tool embedding rules (classe éco, 2h avant). *Leg:* lines snap into ruled lanes with an ink-underline draw-on.
- **t=10 — «Le Tarif» / «Des tarifs négociés, choisis pour vous»** On screen: the ticket splits into three overlapping fare cards (Air, Rail, Hotel), the two non-selected fade to 15% ink; a price tag flips. *Leg:* cards shuffle riffle-style, winning card slides to front and gets a red corner fold.
- **t=15 — «Conseiller» / «Un conseiller dédié, pas un robot»** On screen: a rotary-dial phone drawn in ink; the ticket curls into an envelope; a red thread stitches envelope to phone. *Leg:* envelope flies left-to-right along a dotted line that draws itself.
- **t=20 — «En Vol» / «Une équipe 24/7, même à 3h du matin»** On screen: a minimal vector world map, flat; the envelope becomes a tiny plane crossing it; a clock face rotates its hands 12 hours in the background; a small pin pulses. *Leg:* plane leaves a dashed trail; a geolocation ring ripples from the pin.
- **t=25 — «Notes» / «Frais, factures, rapports : tout tombe dedans»** On screen: plane lands, becomes a receipt; receipts, e-invoice glyphs and a bar chart pour into the open booklet like paper into a tray. *Leg:* waterfall of documents stacking with a satisfying one-by-one drop, slight overshoot.
- **t=30 — «Retour» / «Le dossier se ferme, le bilan s'ouvre»** On screen: the full booklet, now stamped 6 times, closes cover-first; the cover stamps itself "ASSET" in red; it shelves into a filing cabinet with 122 drawer slots (Selectour network). *Leg:* camera pulls back revealing the cabinet is a map of France.

**Pinned signature object:** the *passeport de mission* — a fixed booklet whose stamps carry real info: negotiated-fare %, response-time SLA, assistance number, reporting cadence, network badge.

**Drum / approach:** Analyse → Cahier des charges → Réservation → Accompagnement → Bilan

**Why a travel manager remembers it:** because their travel *policy becomes the ink* — every promise ASSET makes arrives as a literal stamp on a document they can close and file.

## CONCEPT 2 — «De la Demande au Tarmac»

**Master metaphor:** The homepage is one departure board at an airport, and every scroll moves a single trip down the board toward takeoff. The metaphor is *the trip itself is the product*: request → itinerary → boarding → runway → sky.

**7-stage film storyboard** (vector: split-flap board typography, tarmac lines, night-ink blue + signal orange accent):

- **t=0 — «Demande» / «Votre besoin, écouté par un humain»** On screen: a split-flap departure board, all tiles blank, flipping chaotically; tiles lock one by one into "PARIS ISSY → VOTRE OBJECTIF". *Leg:* flap-clatter decelerates, board slides up to reveal tarmac floor lines below.
- **t=5 — «Itinéraire» / «Un trajet dessiné pour votre budget»** On screen: the board's row stretches into a horizontal route line; three nodes appear (train, plane, hotel); inked route curves swap until one is cheapest. *Leg:* nodes connect with an elastic line that overshoots and settles; a € tag ticks down.
- **t=10 — «Billet» / «Réservé, conforme à votre politique»** On screen: a boarding pass prints from the bottom of frame, dot-matrix style; small policy icons (✓ classe éco, ✓ hôtel 3★) get punched into its stub. *Leg:* perforation line draws itself with a tear-jiggle.
- **t=15 — «Bagages» / «Vous partez léger, nous portons le reste»** On screen: the boarding pass shrinks into a suitcase; icons for *frais, assurances, visas, factures* fold into the case like shirts. *Leg:* suitcase latches click shut (two small ink ticks), handle extends.
- **t=20 — «Tarmac» / «Sur la piste, avec le réseau Selectour»** On screen: suitcase rolls right along runway dashes toward a vector plane; behind, 122 tiny runway lights light up across a France silhouette (the network). *Leg:* plane's turbines spin up as rotating ink ellipses; camera tracks right with the suitcase.
- **t=25 — «Décollage» / «Assistance 24/7 : nous restons au sol, pour vous»** On screen: plane lifts off, leaving the suitcase silhouette behind — revealing the trip's paperwork stays with ASSET; a red "24/7" radar sweep replaces the sun. *Leg:* plane exits top-right; a dotted geolocation trace stays projected on the map below.
- **t=30 — «Retour» / «Le bilan atterrit sur votre bureau»** On screen: plane returns, drops a folded report parachute; it lands on a desk outline next to a coffee-cup glyph (no people). *Leg:* parachute folds into a bar-chart page with a red "Prochaine mission?" flap.

**Pinned signature object:** the **boarding pass header** — a fixed ticket-shaped rail that accumulates the seven stages as punched stubs; each stub carries a real number (conseiller direct, délai de devis <4h, assistance 24/7, 122 agences).

**Drum / approach:** Écouter → Dessiner → Réserver → Veiller → Rendre compte

**Why a travel manager remembers it:** because it makes tangible the one thing they fear losing — *control*: the policy punches the ticket before the traveller ever boards.

## CONCEPT 3 — «La Carte Se Déplie»

**Master metaphor:** A folded paper map — the oldest travel object — unfolds, and each panel of the fold reveals one stage of the client journey. Scroll literally unfolds the map; by t=30 it is fully open, and the folds' creases turn out to be flight routes.

**7-stage film storyboard** (vector: paper fold mechanics, contour lines, red route ink, Michelin-style type):

- **t=0 — «Repli» / «Un partenaire à taille humaine»** On screen: a small tri-fold map, closed, standing upright like a pamphlet; cover reads "ASSET VOYAGES — ISSY-LES-MOULINEAUX". *Leg:* top third unfolds upward with a paper-flex shadow, revealing France's contour lines.
- **t=5 — «Dépli** / «Analysons votre façon de voyager»** On screen: second panel unfolds left; contour lines resolve into commute routes from Issy radiating toward Paris. *Leg:* a red route ink draws from Issy outward like veins.
- **t=10 — **Tracé** / «Des itinéraires négociés, pas improvisés»** On screen: third panel unfolds right; the red route forks into fare options, cheapest thickens, others go grey. *Leg:* route-ink bulges then settles; small € flags plant along it.
- **t=15 — **Cachet** / «La politique de voyage, imprimée dans le pli»** On screen: a magnifying-glass circle roams the map; inside it, policy clauses appear microprinted along the route like map legend text. *Leg:* the lens enlarges 2× then stamps a red circle.
- **t=20 — **Mouvance** / «Vos équipes voyagent, nous veillons»** On screen: the map tilts to near-flat (2.5D via skewed vector); pins drop where travellers are; a ripple alert rings one pin. *Leg:* pins drop with bounce; one ripple expands over three countries.
- **t=25 — **Regroupement** / «Séminaires, congrès : 10 personnes ou 400»** On screen: pins converge into cluster shapes forming an abstract group glyph; a compass rose rotates to "MICE"; the map reveals a second layer of congress-city dots worldwide. *Leg:* dots blink on like city lights, one by one, fastest rhythm of the film.
- **t=30 — **Ouverture** / «La carte complète. Le monde, géré localement.»** On screen: final fold opens — the map is now a full world map; all red routes crease-connect back to Issy, one origin point. *Leg:* routes retract simultaneously into the origin like strings; the cover crease re-prints "Selectour Affaires · 122 agences".

**Pinned signature object:** the **folding map rail** — a fixed accordion-fold strip along the viewport edge; each fold flap is a stage and flips open a real data panel (rates, SLA, geolocation, catalogue).

**Drum / approach:** Analyse → Cahier des charges → Réussite → Accompagnement → Bilan

**Why a travel manager remembers it:** because "human-sized agency, worldwide reach" stops being a slogan and becomes a literal image — every route creased back to one desk in Issy.

## What I'd ship

**«De la Demande au Tarmac»** — because the departure-board/split-flap language is instantly legible to every buyer persona (SME owner, travel manager, HR, congress participant) with zero explanation, and its motion vocabulary (flap, punch, roll, takeoff) gives us seven mechanically distinct keyframes that stay exciting across repeated scrubs. The boarding-pass rail is the strongest *packable* signature object: it accumulates real proof-points visually, which is exactly what a procurement-minded travel manager screenshots for their boss. The other two are beautiful but Concept 2 uniquely sells ASSET's core differentiator — *we hold the paperwork while you fly* — which is the emotional truth of a human-sized agency in one gesture.
