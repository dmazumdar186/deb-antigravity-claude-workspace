# Market pricing research - Paris/France, as of 2026-10-28

Method: WebSearch summaries plus one successful WebFetch (Codeur). Malt, La Fabrique du Net and Codeur-style pages returned 403 on direct fetch, so most figures below come from search-engine summaries of those pages; verify before quoting to the client. Exact publication dates were mostly not shown; "date" = year/month label shown on the page. Anything not listed = not found.
