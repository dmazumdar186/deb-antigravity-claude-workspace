1 Two options, both working

You asked to see how we would handle congresses, flight enquiries and GM&E codes. Rather than describe it, we built it twice, so you can try it yourselves and pick the option that suits ASSET.

Both demos are built to the same five requirements from your consultation. Every congress, person, enquiry and code in them is fictional. Each demo is a sandbox, a safe practice copy: it resets on its own, so nothing you click can break anything.

2 Try it yourself

Your five requirements, one task each. Each task shows where to click in Option A and in Option B. Tasks 3 and 4 follow on from each other: send an enquiry as a visitor, then find it as the team.

  1. Task 1 Create or edit a congress

    Your team, in the admin

    Option A: WordPress

    1. Open the admin. You are already signed in.
    2. In the left-hand menu, choose « Congrès », then « Ajouter un congrès ».
    3. Fill in the name, dates, city and discipline, then publish. To change one, open it from « Tous les congrès ».

    Sign in as contributeur instead and your congress waits for review before it goes live.

    Option B: custom platform

    1. Open the team console and sign in as contributeur.
    2. Open the list of congresses and click « Ajouter un congrès », or open an existing one to change it.
    3. Send it for review. Sign in as direction to check it and publish it.

  2. Task 2 Find a congress in the public catalogue

    Your visitors, on the public site

    Option A: WordPress

    1. Open the visitor site and go to the congress catalogue.
    2. Type a word in the search box, or narrow the list by discipline, country or month.
    3. Open a congress to see its details page: dates, city, venue and discipline.

    Option B: custom platform

    1. Open the visitor site and go to the congress catalogue.
    2. Type a word in the search box, or narrow the list by discipline, country or month.
    3. Open a congress to see its details page: dates, city, venue and discipline.

  3. Task 3 Send a flight enquiry

    Your visitors, on the public site

    Option A: WordPress

    1. On a congress details page, find « Demander un devis vol ».
    2. Enter your contact details, departure city, travel dates and number of passengers.
    3. Click « Envoyer ma demande ». You receive a reference number on screen.

    Try leaving a field empty, or a return date before the outward date: the form says what to correct.

    Option B: custom platform

    1. On a congress details page, find « Demander un devis vol ».
    2. Enter your contact details, departure city, travel dates and number of passengers.
    3. Click « Envoyer ma demande ». You receive a reference number on screen.

    The same checks apply: the form says what to correct before anything is sent.

  4. Task 4 Receive, view, track and update enquiries

    Your team, in the admin

    Option A: WordPress

    1. In the same tab as task 3, click « Espace équipe » and sign in as equipe. Open « Demandes »: your enquiry is at the top.
    2. Open it and change its status, for example from « Nouvelle » to « Qualifiée ». Each change is kept in its history.
    3. Click « Exporter en CSV » to download the list as a spreadsheet file. « Journal des notifications » shows the message your team would have received.

    Option B: custom platform

    1. Open the team console, sign in as equipe and open « Demandes ».
    2. Open your enquiry from task 3 and change its status. Each change is kept in its history.
    3. Click « Exporter en CSV » to download the list as a spreadsheet file. « Journal des notifications » shows the message your team would have received.

  5. Task 5 Protect the GM&E codes

    Your team and management

    GM&E codes are the Air France-KLM Global Meetings & Events fare codes for a congress. Your team sees them hidden and asks for access, giving a reason. Direction approves for 24 hours, or refuses. Each time a code is shown, it is recorded. Visitors never see a code, only a line saying that a negotiated congress fare is available.

    Option A: WordPress

    1. Signed in as equipe, open « Codes GM&E › Codes »: the codes are hidden. Click « Demander l’accès » and give a reason.
    2. Sign out, sign in as direction, open « Codes GM&E › Demandes d’accès », and click « Approuver pour 24 h » or « Refuser ».
    3. Sign in as equipe again and open « Codes GM&E › Codes ». The code stays hidden until you click « Afficher le code »: that click is needed. Each display is then listed in « Journal d’accès GM&E ».

    Option B: custom platform

    1. In the team console, sign in as equipe and open « Codes GM&E › Codes »: the codes are hidden. Click « Demander l’accès » and give a reason.
    2. Sign out, sign in as direction, open « Codes GM&E › Demandes d’accès », and click « Approuver pour 24 h » or « Refuser ».
    3. Sign in as equipe again and open « Codes GM&E › Codes ». The code stays hidden until you click « Afficher le code »: that click is needed. Each display is then listed in « Journal d’accès GM&E ».

One more idea: a link for group trips

For a group going to a congress, each traveller’s details often arrive by e-mail and are copied into a spreadsheet by hand.

What it does

  1. Your team sends one link for the enquiry, in its reply to the client.
  2. Each traveller fills in their own details on a phone: name as on the ID document, e-mail, departure city and dates.
  3. Your team sees the list fill in, with a « répondus / attendus » counter and a ready-to-use export, « Exporter pour cotation (CSV) ».

How to try it

  1. Open enquiry DV-2026-0001 in the Option B console (new tab) and sign in as equipe, password demo-asset.
  2. Choose the « Groupe » tab. Three fictional travellers have already answered.
  3. Under « Lien de groupe », copy the link and open it in another tab. This is the traveller’s page.
  4. Send an answer, then go back to the « Groupe » tab: it is in the list.

The traveller’s page never shows the other travellers, codes or fares.

Where it stands

  • Working in Option B’s demonstration, with fictional travellers.
  • Suggestion only. It is not part of the quotation and changes nothing in the scope or budget.
  • In WordPress, it would be built as part of the project, if you choose it.

Honest limits of the demo

  • A traveller who changes phone cannot correct their entry directly: the change waits for your team to accept it. Production would add an e-mail confirmation step.
  • Anyone the link is forwarded to can answer. Revoking it stops that at once.
  • Reminders by e-mail and a status per traveller come in production.

4 How each one is built, in plain words

Option A: WordPress

  • WordPress, the system your current site already runs on.
  • Our own plugin (an add-on to WordPress), with no page builders and no paid plugins.
  • Your team edits in the standard WordPress editor.
  • Hosted with a European WordPress host, in ASSET’s name.
  • Needs regular updates of WordPress and its plugins.

Option B: our custom platform

  • The public pages are prepared in advance and served from Cloudflare’s global network.
  • A small console for your team.
  • The database is kept in the European Union.
  • Staff sign in with a code sent to their email.
  • No plugins to update; the codes are kept in a separate, locked table.

How to choose

Choose A if a familiar editor and the widest choice of future developers matter most.

Choose B if the least upkeep and security designed in from the start matter most.

With either one: the source code, the data and every account are in ASSET’s name; you can export everything; and you receive documentation.

6 Milestones

Counted from your approval and receipt of the required content. The plan is the same for both options.

  1. Kickoff, access and audit

    Weeks 1 to 2

    An audit of your current site, and one workshop of up to 2 hours.

  2. Design

    Weeks 2 to 5

    Wireframes (page outlines), then desktop and mobile mock-ups, with two rounds of changes at each stage.

  3. Development

    Weeks 5 to 10

    Pages, the congress catalogue, enquiries, GM&E access, and the import of your congress list, with a trial run first.

  4. Testing

    Weeks 10 to 12

    On a private copy of the site, against your acceptance tests T01 to T15, then fixes.

  5. Training

    Week 12

    For your team, with a second session four weeks after launch.

  6. Launch

    Week 13

    After your written acceptance and management’s go-ahead, with old addresses redirected to the new pages.

  7. Stabilisation

    Four weeks after launch

    We watch the new site closely and fix anything that comes up.

If the year-end holidays fall inside the plan, those weeks are added. The launch date is agreed together.
