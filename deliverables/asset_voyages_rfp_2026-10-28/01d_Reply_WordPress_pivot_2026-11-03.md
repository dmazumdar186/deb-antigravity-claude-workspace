All the items you list are in: the Business Travel, MICE and International Congress sections; a Congress catalogue that your team manages on its own (add, edit, import, approve, archive, with the internal fare workflow kept private); the consulting page on corporate travel cost optimisation; the ASSET Savings Scan enquiry form and a presentation of the future SmartFees offers (presentation only, no subscription billing); and the Wondermiles presentation page with a corporate account enquiry form and a link to your existing booking platform. **2. Fixed price, allowances and what is included**
The proposal will state a fixed price of €3,000 excluding VAT, with a precise allowance table: number of pages and templates, the five enquiry forms, two design revision rounds, migration of your existing pages, and the import of up to 300 congress listings from a spreadsheet template we supply, checked for duplicates and missing fields. Anything beyond the allowances is listed with its price before it starts, so the fixed price holds.

**3. Milestones, technologies and additional charges**
The proposal will give dated milestones from signature to launch, the list of technologies and plugins (WordPress with a small plugin of our own for the catalogue, roles and enquiry handling, and free, widely used plugins for forms, security, backups, SEO and languages, so there are no recurring licence fees in the base build), and a short list of additional charges with their trigger.

**4. Hosting, backups, security, updates, plugin licences, assistance and bug fixes**
These are itemised separately in the proposal. Hosting, backups and any plugin licence are contracted and paid by ASSET directly, in ASSET's name, at the provider's price; we recommend a French host and set everything up as part of the build. Assistance and bug fixes are offered as an optional monthly care plan, with no minimum term, and as an hourly rate for ad hoc requests. Corrections of anything in the agreed scope that fails the acceptance tests are included at no cost.

ASSET owns the website, its content and data, and the custom code we write, with full administrator access from the first day. Documentation, a handover session and a written handover record are included; at handover we remove our own access.

**6. Managing the site yourselves or with another developer**
That is how it is designed. WordPress is the most widely supported platform there is; any WordPress developer can take over from the documentation and the administrator account. No maintenance contract is required. One point we state plainly in the proposal: after handover, security updates are the responsibility of whoever maintains the site, whether that is your team, another developer, or us under the optional care plan.

On references: our recent production work has been on modern static platforms rather than WordPress, and as a matter of policy we do not name our clients in proposals.
