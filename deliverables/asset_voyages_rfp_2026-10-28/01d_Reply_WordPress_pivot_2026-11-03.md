The proposal states the allowance table precisely: pages and templates, forms, revision rounds, migration, the congress import, training hours. Within the allowances the price does not move. Dated milestones from signature to launch; WordPress with a small plugin of our own for the catalogue, roles and enquiry handling, and free, widely used plugins for forms, security, backups, SEO and languages, so the base build carries no recurring licence fees; a short list of additional charges with their trigger.

Itemised separately. Hosting, backups and any plugin licence are contracted and paid by ASSET directly, in ASSET's name, at the provider's price; we set them up as part of the build. After handover, security updates are the responsibility of whoever maintains the site, which the proposal states plainly.

ASSET owns the website, its content and data, and the custom code we write, with administrator access from the first day. Documentation, a handover session and a written handover record are included; at handover we remove our own access.

That is the design. Any WordPress developer can take over from the documentation and the administrator account. No maintenance contract is required.
