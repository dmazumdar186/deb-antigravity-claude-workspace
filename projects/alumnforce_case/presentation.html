The insight

In this data, "three years in post" and "three years without an update" are the same signal

People update their profile when they start a job (median 112 days later) and then go silent. A naive assistant answering the brief's own example returns exactly the people most likely to have moved.

That is the 340-person London e-mail disaster, reproduced on their own file.

Measure, money, risk

How we know it works, and what pays for it

Stop criteria

Sentence box stops at week 12 if under 30 % of new segments use it or admins rewrite more than half of what it produces.

Reactivation stops at week 17 if fewer than 10 % of "to reactivate" profiles come back.

Money

Reliability included for everyone: the renewal argument for 350 tenants.

Sentence box included with fair use. Only the reactivation loop is priced, because only it is tied to dues collected.

Three risks, three positions

Dirty data: the reliability layer is the product.

DPO veto: no member data in any prompt, ever.
